---
title: The old place
date: 2026-09-21
---

Had a dream last night that I was back in the Logan Square apartment. Everything was exactly where it used to be: the plants on the windowsill, the rug with the coffee stain, the table in the kitchen with a coaster folded under the short leg. The lights were on and music was playing, like someone had just stepped out.

I kept walking from room to room looking for something, I don't know what. Marissa wasn't there, but her coat was on the hook. At one point I sat down at the table and ran my hand along the top, and it was warm, like someone had just been sitting there. I remember thinking, "Oh good, I didn't lose it." Then my alarm went off.

I woke up sad and stayed sad all morning, but I couldn't say what about. It wasn't really missing Marissa. It was closer to that feeling when you walk into a room and forget why you came in. Like I'd left something important there and couldn't remember what it was.

Work was busy, which helped. Still thinking about the table.
