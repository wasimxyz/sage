title: Thursday trivia
date: 2026-09-24
---

Jason posted a photo from trivia tonight. The whole crew squeezed into the corner booth at the Owl, holding up the answer sheet because they finally won. First place. They were stuck in third for two years, and the first time they win, I'm 2,000 miles away eating leftover noodles.

Marissa was in it. End of the booth, laughing at something off camera, wearing the green sweater I bought her on the Michigan trip. I zoomed in, which I'm not proud of. I don't know why I expected her not to be there. She skipped my goodbye night, so I guess I figured she'd stepped back. But they were her friends too, probably more hers than mine. Of course she'd go back once I was gone.

The first feeling was jealousy, and it wasn't even about her. It was jealousy of everyone in that booth. They have each other, and the Owl, and a Thursday that still means something. I'm jealous of a corner booth. That's where I'm at.

I'm not mad at her. I'm glad they still have each other. It's more that the photo made something obvious that I'd been avoiding. The life I left didn't stop when I left. It just closed up over the spot where I used to be. Everyone's still there doing Thursdays, and I'm here refreshing a photo.

Then I scrolled up through my texts with Jason, and that part was worse. He's texted me five times since I moved. A link to a dumb video. "How's the rain." "Call me when you're free, want to hear about the new job." A photo of a dog he swears looks like me. "You alive?" I answered two of them, both with about four words.

That's when the shame came in, and it's still here. Jason asked me to be his best man a few months ago. He's planning a wedding and has a million things going on, and he's still the one reaching out every week. I'm the one who went quiet. I can't tell myself they forgot about me.

I've been telling myself I'd call once things were better. I didn't want to call him just to be sad at him. Every time I thought about it, I imagined him asking how Seattle is, and me not having anything good to say, and him hearing it in my voice. So I'd put it off until tomorrow, and then tomorrow I'd still have nothing good to say.

I liked the photo. Typed "congrats!! finally" and deleted it. Typed "miss you guys" and deleted that too. Ended up not commenting anything.

It's 11pm and I'm still up. Chicago is two hours ahead, so by the time I feel brave enough to call anyone, everyone there is asleep.
