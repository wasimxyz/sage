---
title: Bad week
date: 2026-10-16
---

Bad week. I'm writing this at the counter at 10pm with a bag of chips for dinner, so that should tell you how it's going.

The sign-up flow was supposed to go out to everyone next week. On Wednesday morning, testing found a bug where people on older phones get stuck on step four with no way to go back, and since step four is my screen, it became my problem. I don't think it was really my fault. The engineers say it's something about how the screen loads. But I spent all of Wednesday and Thursday in meetings about it, and yesterday the launch got pushed two weeks.

Priya has been stressed and short with everyone, me included. On Wednesday she said, "I need you thinking harder about edge cases," in front of the whole team. It was a fair note, and she messaged me later to apologize for how she said it. It still sat in my stomach for two days. At the studio, Rosa would have just walked over to my desk. Here it was a video call with fourteen people on it.

So last night at 6:15, I texted Dev: "So sorry man, work's on fire, can't make trivia tonight." Which was true, sort of. I was still at work. But I could have left by 7. I didn't want to sit in a bar being the guy whose launch just got delayed. When I hit send, part of me felt relieved, and I didn't look at that too closely.

Dev wrote back in about thirty seconds: "No worries!! Next week. Sam says we need you, we came in dead last again," with a skull emoji. And I got annoyed. At Dev. For being nice. I read it and felt this little flash of irritation, like, why are you being so easy about this? Which makes no sense. He did everything right.

I think his being nice made it worse. If he'd been annoyed, I could've felt like we were even. Instead he just let me off the hook, and it made me feel like a flake who doesn't deserve a friend who's that easygoing. The bowl was sitting right next to my phone when I read it. I put a dish towel over it, which is the most dramatic thing I've ever done to a piece of pottery.

Today I mostly felt numb. Not sad, not stressed, just nothing. I did my work, sat in two more bug meetings, said "sounds good" a lot, and took the bus home. Halfway home I realized I hadn't said a real sentence to anyone all day that wasn't about step four.

I recognize this. It's the going quiet thing. Jason described it to me a week ago, how I disappear when things go bad, and here I am doing it again. I haven't texted him back about the venue. I haven't answered Dev. I've eaten at the counter every night this week, with the bowl under a towel.

I don't fully understand why this week hit so hard. A delayed launch is normal. A bug is normal. A slightly harsh comment in a meeting is extremely normal. But it's felt like everything got dimmer at once, and I can't find the switch.

Tomorrow's Saturday. Tomorrow's also one year since Marissa ended things. I'm trying not to think about that.
