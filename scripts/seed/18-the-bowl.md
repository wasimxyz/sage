---
title: The bowl
date: 2026-10-13
---

Got the bowl back tonight. Walt brought out a tray of last week's pieces after they came out of the kiln, and mine was easy to find because it was the ugliest one. It's lopsided, the thumbprint is still on the rim, and the dark blue glaze I picked pooled at the bottom and ran down one side like it was trying to escape.

I love it. I don't know what's wrong with me. I held it for a long time, turning it around, and I felt proud in a way I haven't felt in months. Prouder than when the VP asked who designed the sign-up flow. It's a bowl. A bad one. I made it in an hour and a half, and most of what I did was ruin clay.

Dev's came out better than mine, which he would like me to write down. It's a mug with a handle slightly too small for any human finger. He named it "the espresso, technically." We sat next to each other again, because he really did save me a wheel. His jacket was on the stool before I got there.

This week Walt had us try taller pieces. I was bad at it, but less bad than last week. At one point he stood behind me watching and said, "You've got good hands, chief. You done this before?" I said no, but I used to do woodworking. He said, "Yeah, I can tell. You're not scared of the material." I've been thinking about that sentence ever since, and I'm not sure why it hit me so hard. It was a compliment about clay.

I almost got teary on the bus home with the bowl in my lap wrapped in a paper towel, which is embarrassing to write down. I kept trying to name the feeling. It wasn't happiness exactly. It was more like spotting someone across a room that you haven't seen in years, right before you remember their name.

After class Dev said his roommate Sam does trivia every Thursday at a bar near their place called the Pinewood, and asked if I wanted to come this week. I said yes before I could think about it. My first Thursday plan in Seattle.

The bowl is on the counter now, next to where I eat. I put my keys in it. It wobbles a little when I drop them in, which feels right somehow, though I can't explain why.
