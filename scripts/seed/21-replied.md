---
title: Replied
date: 2026-10-18
---

Told Jason about the text on our call this morning. He listened to the whole thing, all four feelings, and then said, "So what do you actually want to say back?" I said I didn't know. He said, "You do. You just don't like it because it's simple."

So after we hung up I wrote: "Thanks, M. It means a lot that you reached out. It's getting better here, slowly. I hope you're happy." I read it once and sent it before I could start editing. She replied an hour later with "I am. I'm glad it's getting better," and a heart.

And that was it. I felt lighter, like I'd put down a bag I'd been carrying since the move. I also felt a little sad that it was that easy. A year of dreading this, a letter I'll never send, and the whole thing took four sentences. Part of me wanted it to be harder, like that would prove it had mattered.

Mom called tonight and said I sounded "like myself." I didn't tell her why.
