---
title: Thursday
date: 2026-08-20
---

It's Thursday. I didn't think about it until around six, when I was standing in the kitchen eating cereal for dinner and my phone buzzed with the group chat. Trivia starts at eight back home. Kat had sent a photo of the answer sheet with "TEAM NAME?????" written across the top. We always fight about the team name. I typed three ideas and deleted all of them.

Then something hit me that I'm pretty sure was homesickness. I've never really had it before. In college I was two hours from home and went back every other weekend. This was different. It felt physical, like a weight in the middle of my chest. My throat got tight, and for a minute I had to put the bowl down and hold onto the counter.

What surprised me is how specific it was. I didn't miss Chicago in general. I missed the sticky table at the Owl, and Omar reading every question out loud a second time even though we all heard it, and Jason saying "trust me" right before he's wrong. I missed knowing exactly where I'd be at 8pm on a Thursday, and who would be there.

I've been here twelve days. Work is fine and busy, mostly setup and meetings where people say "let's take that offline." The apartment has boxes in it now instead of nothing. On paper I'm doing okay. But I don't have anywhere I'm supposed to be tonight, and nobody here would notice if I didn't show up anywhere.

I thought about calling Jason, but he was at trivia. Then I thought about calling Mom, but she'd hear it in my voice and worry, and I'd spend the whole call convincing her I'm fine. So I called nobody and watched two episodes of a cooking show instead.

I'm writing this down because I want to remember what it felt like, so that later, when this city feels like home, I can look back and see how far I've come. That's the plan, anyway.
