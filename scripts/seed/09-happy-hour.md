---
title: Happy hour
date: 2026-09-15
---

Priya posted in the team channel this morning that she's taking everyone out for drinks on Thursday to celebrate the first round of sign-up tests. There's a bar near the office with a back patio, apparently, and the company is paying. Eleven people reacted with the beer emoji within ten minutes.

She messaged me directly after. "You're coming, right? Would be a good way to get to know people outside of standups." I wrote back, "Ahh, I have plans Thursday, next time for sure!" I don't have plans Thursday. I have the same plans I have every night, which is dinner at the counter and something on my laptop.

The second I sent it, I felt relief. Real, full-body relief, like when a meeting gets canceled. My shoulders actually dropped. Then about a minute later the shame showed up. I moved here knowing nobody, I've been writing in this journal about being lonely, and when someone literally invites me to spend time with a group of people, I lie to get out of it.

I can't tell which feeling is the real one. Part of me thinks the relief is the truth, that I just don't want to go, and the shame is me thinking I should want to. Another part thinks the shame is the truth, and the relief is just me being scared.

If I picture going, what I see is two hours of the same questions from my first week. Why Seattle, how's the move, do you like it here. Me saying "it's great, honestly" and doing the voice. I'm pretty good at the voice, but it wipes me out. After lunch with the team on my first day, I went home and slept for two hours.

It's also on a Thursday. I don't know if that matters. It shouldn't. But Thursday is trivia night, and going to a bar with a bunch of near-strangers on a Thursday feels like replacing something, or pretending it's the same thing when it isn't.

Next time I'll go. I actually mean that. I just need to be a little more settled first, so I'm not the new guy who's still figuring everything out.
