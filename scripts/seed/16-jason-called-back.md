---
title: Jason called back
date: 2026-10-08
---

Jason called back tonight, two days after my voicemail, right before trivia, from his car in the lot behind the Owl. When his name came up on my screen my stomach dropped, and I almost let it ring out. I don't even know what I was afraid of. That he'd be mad, maybe. Or worse, that he'd be polite.

He wasn't either. The first thing he said was, "Okay, I need to hear more about this sad pancake." I laughed, and then I started apologizing and couldn't stop. I said I was sorry for the texts, for disappearing, for being a bad best man before the wedding has even happened. I was so embarrassed I was walking circles around the apartment.

He let me go for a while, and then he said, "Ben. You don't have to have good news to call me. You know that, right?" I said I know. He said, "No, I don't think you do." Then he reminded me that after Marissa left, I did the same thing. Went quiet for almost two months and answered everything with "doing okay, man." He only found out how bad it was because Leah ran into me at the grocery store and told him I looked like a ghost.

I'd honestly forgotten that. Or I'd remembered it differently, like I'd been taking some space. Hearing him say it out loud, it sounded a lot less like space and a lot more like hiding.

Then he said there was something he figured I should hear from him and not from a photo. Marissa's seeing someone. A guy named Nate, from her climbing gym. It's been a few months. She brought him to trivia two weeks ago, the night they won. He was the one who took the picture.

I said, "Oh. Cool. Good for her." And I meant it, kind of. I also felt like I'd missed a step on the stairs. I don't know what I feel about it yet. I'm putting it here so I can figure it out later.

We talked for almost an hour. He told me about the wedding, which they've moved to Leah's parents' place in Wisconsin. He's counting on me for the toast and won't accept one written the night before. Omar got a dog. He asked if I'd fly back for their engagement party in about seven weeks, and I said I'd think about it, which he said was a no in a trench coat. We agreed to talk every Sunday. He put it in his calendar while we were on the phone and made me do it too.

When we hung up, I sat down on the kitchen floor and cried. Not sad crying, or not only. Mostly it was relief, so big it didn't fit anywhere. I didn't realize how much I'd been bracing for him to be done with me until it was obvious he wasn't.

After, I went back and reread "Thursday trivia." I'd written that the life I left "just closed up over the spot where I used to be." Reading it tonight, that's not what happened. Jason had been holding the spot open the whole time. Five texts. I was the one who didn't walk back into it.

I also reread the part where I said I didn't want to call him "just to be sad at him." I think I had it backwards. He didn't need me to be fine. He needed me to call.
