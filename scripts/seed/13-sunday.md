---
title: Sunday
date: 2026-09-27
---

I didn't say a single word out loud today until 4pm, when the guy at the grocery store asked if I wanted a bag. I said "no thanks" and my voice came out scratchy, like I'd just woken up. That's pretty much been the whole weekend.

Mom called this evening, like she does every Sunday. Within a minute she said, "Benny, you sound tired." I said I'd just been reading. She asked if I've been making friends, and I said yeah, a few people from work, which isn't true. Priya invited the whole team to happy hour two weeks ago and I said I had plans.

Then Mom said, "It's so strange to think of you not at Elena's on a Sunday," and caught herself, and said sorry. For three years, Sunday dinner at Marissa's sister's place was just a fact of my life, the same way Mom's calls are. Mom used to love hearing who brought what and who argued about what. I think she misses it a little too.

Then she asked if I'd bought a table yet, because last time I mentioned I eat standing at the counter, and she thinks a 32-year-old eating dinner standing up is the saddest thing she's ever heard. I told her I'm looking.

The thing about the table is I already had one. I built it the first winter Marissa and I lived together, at the community woodshop on Milwaukee Ave. White oak, almost three months of Saturdays. I cut one of the legs a little short so it wobbled, and Marissa used to fold a coaster under it and call it "the signature." When she moved out, she asked if she could take it, and I said yes right away. It felt like the least I could do. It was also the one thing in that apartment that felt like mine, the one thing I'd made with my own hands, and I gave it away in about four seconds.

I haven't built anything since. My tools are in a box in the closet here, still taped shut. I don't think I've made anything at all, unless you count sign-up flows, which I guess I'm supposed to count. Making things is literally my job. It just doesn't feel like making something when it lives in a Figma file and four other people change it before it ships.

I tried to think of the last thing that actually felt like it mattered. Not important, just mattered to me. I couldn't come up with anything from this year. The clinic site, maybe, but that was over a year ago. I don't love that answer.

On Tuesday I walked to the pottery studio. I actually went. The lights were on, there were maybe eight people inside at the wheels, music going, everyone laughing about something. I stood across the street for a minute, and then I kept walking like I'd been heading somewhere else the whole time. Mostly I felt embarrassed, like everyone inside would be able to tell I'd come alone because I didn't have anyone to come with.

I keep thinking about what I'm working toward right now. I get up, I work, I come home, I watch something, I go to sleep. None of it is bad. But if someone asked me what I'm building toward, I wouldn't have an answer.

The drop-in is Tuesday again. I put it in my calendar this time, which feels like a weirdly big step for a pottery class.
