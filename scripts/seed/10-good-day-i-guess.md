---
title: Good day, I guess
date: 2026-09-18
---

Had what should count as a good day. I presented the new sign-up flow to the leadership team, and afterward Priya messaged me to say it went great and that the VP asked who designed it. A year ago a message like that would have made my whole week.

Instead I closed my laptop at 6 and felt nothing. Not sad, not tired exactly. Flat. Like someone turned the volume down on the whole day.

I tried to figure out why on the bus home. The work is good. I worked hard on it. The new flow cuts the number of people who give up at step three, and the tests look strong. But when I try to say why that matters, I get as far as "fewer people drop off at step three," and then I run out. It's true. It's just not a reason to get out of bed.

At the studio it was different. When we finished the booking site for the community clinic on Fullerton, one of the nurses emailed Rosa to say their front desk wasn't drowning in phone calls anymore, and that an older patient had booked her own appointment for the first time. Rosa printed the email and taped it to the fridge, and I walked past it every day for a year. Here, the result of my work is a number on a dashboard, and four people I've never met will change the design before it ships.

Maybe that's just what working at a big company is like. Everyone here seems fine with it. I'm probably just still adjusting.

The weekend's coming and I have nothing planned, and not in the relaxing way. More like Saturday is a long empty hallway. I keep telling myself I should sign up for something. The pottery flyer is still on my phone.

Ate dinner standing at the counter again. I should buy a table.
