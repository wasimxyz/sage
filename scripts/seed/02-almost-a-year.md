---
title: Almost a year
date: 2026-09-09
---

Can't sleep, so I'm writing. I was making dinner tonight with a random playlist on, and the song from the Michigan trip came on. The one Marissa played four times in a row on the drive up to Sleeping Bear, until I begged her to pick anything else, and she laughed and played it a fifth time. I stood at the stove holding a wooden spoon and didn't move until it was over.

I did the math after. It'll be a year in October. A year since she sat on the edge of our bed and said she'd felt like my roommate for a long time, and that she thought I felt the same way. I remember being angry that she said it like it was obvious. I think I was angry because it was.

The weird part is I don't think I want her back. I'm pretty sure I don't. We were good for a long time, and then we were just polite, and polite is its own kind of lonely. When I picture calling her, I don't picture us getting back together. I picture her asking how I'm doing, and me actually knowing the answer.

Maybe what I miss is the life around her. Sunday mornings at the farmers market. Dinners at her sister's place where everyone talked over each other. Thursday trivia at the Owl with Jason and the crew, where Marissa was always weirdly good at geography. My week had a whole shape to it, and she was in the middle of it. When she left I didn't just lose her. I lost the shape.

I told everyone I took the Seattle job because it was a great opportunity, and it was. But if I'm honest, the timing wasn't an accident. Every street in Logan Square had something on it. I figured if I moved far enough away, I'd get to start over clean.

Except I brought myself with me. Same guy, new zip code, and now nobody around who knew him before.

Going to try to sleep. Didn't call Jason again this week. Tomorrow.
