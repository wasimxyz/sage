---
title: First Sunday call
date: 2026-10-11
---

It's rained every day this week. Not the dramatic kind. Just a low gray lid over the city and a drizzle that never really starts and never really stops. Everyone at work acts like this is normal, which I guess it is here. Priya told me the trick is a really good raincoat. I bought one.

First Sunday call with Jason. It was short, maybe twenty minutes, because Leah's parents were coming over to talk about the wedding. He told me about the venue tour, and I told him the bowl comes out of the kiln on Tuesday. It was small and normal, and I was in a better mood for about an hour afterward.

Then Mom called at her usual time. She said I sounded better than last week. I told her I'd started a pottery class, and she got so excited that I didn't have the heart to tell her it's been exactly one class. She wants a bowl. I told her she does not want this bowl.

Around 4 the good mood wore off, and I got that heavy, nothing-to-do feeling again. So I went back and reread "Sunday" from two weeks ago, the one where I didn't say a word out loud until the grocery store. Then I scrolled back and found "Seattle so far," which I also wrote on a Sunday, the day the farmers market made me feel flat for no reason.

It's Sundays. That's my worst day. I'd never put it together until I saw the entries lined up. Saturdays I can fill with errands. Weekdays I have work. But on Sundays I wake up and the whole day just sits there, and by the afternoon I feel worse than on any other day of the week.

I don't totally know why. Maybe because there's no work to hide in. Maybe because everyone else seems to have somewhere to be, brunch or family or the market. In Chicago, Sunday was actually my busiest day. The market in the morning, cooking in the afternoon, dinner at Elena's at night. I never had to think about what to do with one. Maybe that's part of it. I'm not sure.

Anyway, now I know. Two parts of Sunday are fixed: Jason in the morning and Mom in the evening. The middle is the part I still have to figure out.
