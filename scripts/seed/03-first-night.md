---
title: First night
date: 2026-08-08
---

First night in Seattle. The apartment is completely empty except for an air mattress, a suitcase, and a pizza box. The truck doesn't come until Tuesday. When I got the keys this afternoon, light was pouring through the big window and I could see a slice of water past the rooftops, and I actually said "oh wow" out loud to nobody. I felt hopeful. Like this could be a real start.

Then around ten I got hit with a wave of panic, and I don't know what set it off. My heart was pounding and my hands went cold. I sat on the air mattress and scrolled through my contacts looking for someone to text, and every single name was in Chicago or Milwaukee. Two thousand miles of people who know me, and nobody here. If something went wrong tonight, there's no one I could call who could actually get to me.

I texted Jason "made it." He sent back a photo of his passenger seat with a fry still on it from the drive to the airport. I laughed, and the panic went down a little.

Going to try to sleep. The air mattress has a slow leak, so by morning I'll probably be on the floor. Which feels about right.
