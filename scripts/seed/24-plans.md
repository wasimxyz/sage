## The part I'm scared of

Looking at that list, I feel hopeful, and the hope makes me nervous. I've been here before. Seven weeks ago I wrote "Seattle so far," a whole list of things I liked, and when I read it back I admitted I'd written it to convince myself. I'm worried this is the same thing. A nicer list with more checkboxes, and the same guy pretending.

I think what scares me is getting my hopes up and watching it fall apart. With Marissa, I let myself believe we'd figure it out eventually, and then one night it was over. Whenever things start going well, part of me waits for the floor to drop out. It's easier not to want much than to want something and lose it.

The difference, maybe, is that the Seattle list was all things that happened to me. The mountain, the ferry, a barista remembering my order. This list is things I have to do. Dev and Sam and Jason are real people expecting me to show up. That's scarier, but it feels less like a postcard.

I'm leaving the engagement party undecided for now. Writing "I don't know" feels more honest than picking one just to make the list look finished.
