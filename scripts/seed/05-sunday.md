title: Sunday
date: 2026-09-27

I didn't say a single word out loud today until 4pm, when the guy at the grocery store asked if I wanted a bag. I said "no thanks" and my voice came out scratchy, like I'd just woken up. That's pretty much been the whole weekend.

Mom called this evening. She asked if I've been making friends, and I said yeah, a few people from work, which isn't true. Priya invited the whole team to happy hour two weeks ago and I said I had plans. Then Mom asked if I'd bought a table yet, because last time we talked I mentioned I eat standing at the counter, and she thinks that's the saddest thing she's ever heard. I told her I'm looking.

The thing about the table is I already had one. I built it the winter Marissa and I moved in together, at the community woodshop on Milwaukee Ave. White oak, almost three months of Saturdays. I cut one of the legs a little short so it wobbled, and Marissa used to fold a coaster under it and call it "the signature." When we split up, it made more sense for her to keep it. It fit her new place, and it wasn't going to fit in a moving truck to Seattle anyway.

I haven't built anything since. I don't think I've made anything at all, unless you count onboarding flows, which I guess I'm supposed to count. Making things is literally my job. It just doesn't feel like making something when it lives in a Figma file and four other people change it before it ships.

I tried to think of the last thing that actually felt like it mattered. Not important, just mattered to me. I couldn't come up with anything from this year. Maybe last summer, before everything. I don't love that answer.

On Tuesday I walked to the pottery studio. I actually went. The lights were on, there were maybe eight people inside at the wheels, music going, everyone laughing about something. I stood across the street for a minute, and then I kept walking like I'd been heading somewhere else the whole time. I don't even know who I was putting that on for.

I keep thinking about what I'm working toward right now. I get up, I work, I come home, I watch something, I go to sleep. None of it is bad. But if someone asked me what I'm building toward, I wouldn't have an answer.

The drop-in is Tuesday again. I put it in my calendar this time, which feels like a weirdly big step for a pottery class.
