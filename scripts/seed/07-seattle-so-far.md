---
title: Seattle so far
date: 2026-09-06
---

Mom asked on the phone today what I actually like about Seattle so far, and I didn't have a good answer ready. It bothered me all afternoon, so here's a list.

- The coffee place on my corner, and the barista who starts my order when I walk in
- The mountain. On clear days Rainier just shows up at the end of the street like it's been there the whole time, which I guess it has
- The ferry. I rode it to Bainbridge last weekend just to ride it back
- How green everything is, even the parking lots
- Nobody honks
- The sliver of water from my window
- Not running into anyone I know

I almost didn't write that last one down, but it's true. In Logan Square I couldn't buy milk without walking past the taco place where Marissa and I had our first date, or the bar where she told her sister we were moving in together. Here nothing is attached to anything. That's a relief. It's also kind of the problem.

I went to the farmers market on Broadway this morning because it seemed like the kind of thing a person who likes their new city would do. I bought a jar of honey and a bunch of flowers I didn't need. It was nice. I don't know why I felt so flat walking home. Everyone there was in pairs or groups, holding coffees and arguing about which bread to get, and I kept thinking I should be enjoying it more than I was.

Reading the list back, I can see what I was doing. I wrote it to convince myself. Every item is true, but it's the kind of true you'd put on a postcard. If Mom asked me what I love about my life right now, I'd probably write the same list, and I'd be lying a little.

I don't know if that makes me ungrateful. I have a good job and a beautiful apartment in a city people would kill to move to. Maybe liking a place is something that happens later, after you have people in it. Maybe I'm just impatient.

The flowers are in a pint glass on the counter because I don't own a vase. They look nice. I'll give the list a month and see.
