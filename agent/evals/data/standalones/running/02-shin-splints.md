Shin splints have gotten bad enough that I finally caved and went to a doctor, who told me flatly that I need to stop running for at least four weeks or risk turning this into a stress fracture. I'm frustrated, mostly at myself for pushing through the pain for as long as I did instead of resting early. Going to try to be disciplined about actually taking the time off instead of sneaking in a few miles when it feels okay.
