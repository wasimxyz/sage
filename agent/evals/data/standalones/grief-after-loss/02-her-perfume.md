---
date: 2025-10-10
title: Her perfume
---

Smelled her perfume today, completely out of nowhere — some woman walking past me on the street was wearing the same one my mother always wore, and it stopped me cold in the middle of the sidewalk. I hadn't thought about that specific smell in months. It's strange what grief decides to attach itself to.

I ended up standing there for a minute, and then just started crying, right there on the street, which hasn't happened to me in public before. It's been seven months. I thought I was past the part where it ambushes me like that. Apparently not.
