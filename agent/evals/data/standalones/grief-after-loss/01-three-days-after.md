date: 2025-03-10
title: Three days after
---

My mother died three days ago and I still haven't cried, not really. I keep waiting for it to feel real. I made all the calls, picked out the flowers, wrote the obituary with my sister at the kitchen table like it was just another task on a list. Everyone keeps telling me I'm being so strong. I don't feel strong. I feel like I'm watching myself do all of this from somewhere outside my body.
