date: 2026-03-07
title: Mom's birthday cake
---

Today would have been Mom's birthday. I made her recipe for the lemon cake, mostly by memory, and it came out wrong — too dense, not enough lemon — and I sat at the counter and laughed and cried at the same time because she would have had so many notes on it.

It's been almost a year now, and I don't think the grief has gotten smaller exactly, just quieter most days, with room in between for things like laughing about a bad cake. I lit a candle and told my sister about it on the phone, and for once talking about Mom didn't just hurt — it felt like something good too.
