title: Jordan proposed
---

Jordan proposed last night, out on the pier where we had our first date four years ago. I'd genuinely had no idea — I thought we were just getting dinner. I said yes before he even finished the sentence, which he still won't let me live down. We stayed out there for an hour just looking at the ring and laughing about nothing. I don't think I've stopped smiling since.
