title: Rent in four days
---

Checked my bank account three times today, which is becoming a habit I don't love. Rent's due in four days and I'm doing that math in my head again — the kind where you're subtracting things you haven't even spent yet just to see how close to zero you'll get. I used to be someone who didn't think about money this much. I don't know when that changed exactly, just that it did.
