title: Built a budget
---

Sat down and actually built a budget for the first time in longer than I want to admit — spreadsheet, categories, the whole thing. It was uncomfortable seeing it all laid out, but less scary than I expected. Turns out most of the anxiety was from not knowing, not from the actual numbers.

I also picked up a few freelance hours on the side, which isn't glamorous but it's something. I don't feel like I'm drowning anymore, more like I'm bailing water at a pace that's actually keeping up. Small thing, but it's the first time in months I've felt like I have some control over this instead of just reacting to it.
