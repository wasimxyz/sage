title: Turned 30
---

Turned 30 today. Spent most of the morning doing the thing where you compare your life to some imaginary checklist you never actually agreed to — should have bought a place by now, should be further along at work, should know more than I do. By the afternoon I'd mostly talked myself down from it.

My friends threw together a small dinner, nothing fancy, and at some point looking around the table I just felt genuinely lucky, checklist aside. Thirty doesn't feel like the milestone I built it up to be. It just feels like a Tuesday with cake.
