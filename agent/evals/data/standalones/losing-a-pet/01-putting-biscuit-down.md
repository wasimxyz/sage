title: Putting Biscuit down
---

Had to put Biscuit down this morning. Fourteen years old, and the vet said his kidneys just couldn't keep up anymore. I keep almost calling his name when I walk in the door, forgetting for a second that the click of his nails on the floor isn't coming. My apartment feels enormous and quiet in a way I wasn't prepared for.
