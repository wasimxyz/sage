title: Missed a deadline
---

Three nights this week I've laid awake running through my inbox instead of sleeping. Sunday night specifically, I get this tight feeling in my chest that doesn't go away until I'm already at my desk staring at the first email. I used to think "Sunday scaries" was something other people exaggerated.

I missed a deadline today — a real one, not a soft one, the kind other people notice. My manager wasn't even harsh about it, and somehow that made it worse. I just sat there afterward feeling like a stranger in my own job.
