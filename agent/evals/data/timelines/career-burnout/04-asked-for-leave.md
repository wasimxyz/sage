title: Asked for leave
---

Talked to my manager today about taking a leave. I'd been rehearsing the conversation for a week, worried it would sound like I was failing, but she just said "I've been wondering when you'd bring this up" — apparently it had been visible for a while, just not to me. We worked out six weeks starting next month.

I feel lighter than I have in months, just from saying it out loud and having someone respond with relief instead of disappointment. I don't know exactly what I'll do with the time yet. I just know I can't keep running on empty and calling it normal.
