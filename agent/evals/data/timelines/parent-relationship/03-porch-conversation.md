date: 2026-02-25
title: The porch conversation
---

Drove up to see Dad this weekend. We ended up talking for almost two hours on the porch — actually talking, not just filling time. It started with him asking about work again, but instead of the usual advice he just got quiet for a second, and then started telling me things I'd never heard before.

He told me about losing his own father young, and how he swore he'd be different with us kids, and then realized somewhere in the middle of actually raising us that he didn't know what different looked like. He said he'd spent a lot of years just repeating what he knew, even when he could feel it wasn't working.

I told him some of that had landed on me anyway — the distance, the way we never really talked about anything real. I expected him to get defensive, the way he usually does when I bring up anything critical. He didn't. He just said he was sorry, and for once it felt like he meant it, not like he was saying it to end the conversation.
