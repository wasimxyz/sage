title: Dad called to check in
---

Dad called just to check in today, no occasion, which he's never really done. We talked for a while about nothing in particular — a project he's working on in the garage, whether I'd been sleeping better. Ordinary stuff, but easy in a way it never used to be.

Before we hung up he said "I love you, kid" — no joke tacked on the end, no rushing past it like he usually does. It's a small thing but I sat in my car for a minute after just letting it land. I kept thinking back to that birthday call a few months ago, how I'd hung up feeling like I'd talked to a coworker. This is a different relationship than the one I grew up with, and I don't think I fully believed it was possible until today.
