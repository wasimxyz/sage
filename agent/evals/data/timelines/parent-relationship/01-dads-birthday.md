title: Dad's birthday
---

Called Dad for his birthday. Same as always — weather, the Giants game, whether I've been getting enough sleep. Forty minutes and I don't think either of us said anything real. I used to think that was just how he was, but lately it's starting to feel more like a wall than a personality. I hung up feeling like I'd talked to a coworker.
