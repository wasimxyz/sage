title: Dad on the phone
---

Something strange happened on the phone with Dad tonight. I mentioned I'd been stressed about work, expecting the usual "you'll figure it out," and instead he got quiet and said he'd felt the same way at my age — that he used to sit in his car in the parking lot before shifts just to breathe. I didn't know what to do with that. He's never told me anything like that before.
