title: Broke up with Theo
---

Theo and I broke up tonight. We'd been circling it for weeks — the fights about scheduling that were never really about scheduling, the long stretches where neither of us reached out first. Sitting across from each other at the kitchen table, it didn't even feel like a fight this time. It felt more like both of us finally saying out loud what we'd been avoiding.

I keep replaying the moment he said "I don't think this is working anymore" and how relieved I felt for about three seconds before the rest of it hit. Two years. I don't know what to do with two years.
