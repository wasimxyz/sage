title: Trying again
---

Theo and I talked for real this weekend, the kind of conversation we should have had before things ended instead of after. We went through it — the scheduling fights, the distance, all of it — and actually tried to name what was underneath instead of just managing the symptoms.

By the end we both said we wanted to try again. Not go back to exactly how things were, but actually try, with the stuff we talked about on the table instead of buried. I'm scared it won't stick. But for the first time since the breakup, I feel like I'm choosing this instead of just missing it.
