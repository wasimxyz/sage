date: 2026-05-13
title: Farmer's market
---

Ran into Theo at the farmer's market today, completely by accident. We ended up getting coffee, which I didn't expect either of us to agree to. It was strange how easy it was to talk to him — easier than it had been in the last few months we were together, actually.

He told me he'd been thinking about what he said that night, that some of it came out wrong, that he wasn't sure ending things was as clear-cut as it felt in the moment. I didn't say much. I mostly listened, and noticed that some of what I'd been carrying around — the anger, mostly — wasn't as loud as I expected it to be, sitting across from him.
