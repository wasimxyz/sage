title: Pumpkins
---

Carved pumpkins at Tess's. Hers was a ghost. Mine was meant to be a ghost and came out as a lumpy potato with a face. Owen didn't carve one, on principle, and told us the principle at length. We roasted the seeds, which came out burned on the outside and raw on the inside, an impressive trick. The pumpkins took up the whole kitchen table, and we ate dinner on the floor.
