title: Reservoir loop
---

Took the bike around the reservoir, eleven miles, and the leaves are starting to turn. Passed a family of five on one of those four-seat pedal carts with the grandmother steering in front. I waved. She did not wave back, she was concentrating. Respect. Stopped at the dam and ate a banana I had been carrying for two days. It was fine.
