title: Dumplings
---

Owen's grandmother's dumpling recipe, which Owen wrote down from a phone call, so the amounts are mostly adjectives. "Enough flour." "A good amount of ginger." We made them anyway, the two of us at my counter, folding for three hours. Mine looked like small crumpled hats. His looked like dumplings. We boiled them and they all tasted the same, which is to say excellent. We ate forty and froze the rest. I now have a bag of crumpled hats in the freezer and I intend to eat every one.
