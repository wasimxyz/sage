title: Monday bus
---

Monday. Took the 14 as usual and stood the whole way because a woman had a stroller and nobody else would move over. Somewhere past the hospital I started thinking what if I stayed on past my stop. Rode it to the end of the line, the transfer center by the highway, then took whatever came next out to the coast. Two hours, less if the buses lined up. Sit on the sea wall with a bucket of fries and my phone off. I got as far as the fries. I had a whole idea of the fries.

I got off at my stop. I'm not going to do it. It's just where my head goes around 7:50 now, most days, out to the sea wall.

At my desk I read the same email three times. At lunch I texted Tess to ask if she wanted to go to the beach the weekend after next. She said she was in. Maybe that's the real answer.
