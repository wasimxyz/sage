title: Thrift store lamp
---

Found a lamp at the thrift store, brass, heavy, about as heavy as a toddler. Four dollars. Carried it home on the bus and got a few looks. Plugged it in and it works, and the light it gives off is yellow and warm. I turned off the overhead and left it on for the evening, and the whole room looked like a different apartment.
