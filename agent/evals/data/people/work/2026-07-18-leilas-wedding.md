---
date: 2026-07-18
title: Leila's wedding
---

My cousin Leila got married today, outside, in a field, with folding chairs that kept sinking into the grass. The drive up took three hours because of a road closure, and I changed shirts in a gas station bathroom with the hand dryer pointed at my collar. Made it with eleven minutes to spare.

Three hundred people, or it felt like it. The ceremony was short. Her husband cried before the vows even started and then everyone else did too, and I had no tissue and ended up using a napkin from a drink I hadn't been handed yet.

My aunt put me at table nine, with the cousins nobody knows what to do with. It was the best table. We toasted ourselves. The dance floor filled up after dinner and stayed full, and I danced to a song from 2008 with my uncle, who has no rhythm and total commitment. Somebody's kid fell asleep under the cake table and was found at eleven, and was fine, and had frosting on his sleeve.

Back at the hotel at one with my shoes in my hand and a slice of cake wrapped in a napkin. The cake is in the hotel fridge. Worth it.
