title: Planning meeting
---

Two hours and forty minutes in the room with no window, going through a spreadsheet nobody had opened beforehand. Somewhere in the second hour I wrote "this meeting is killing me" in the corner of my notebook and underlined it twice. The guy across from me, who I have never heard speak, leaned over, read it upside down, and whispered "get in line." That was the highlight.

Owen texted that night to ask how it went. I wrote that I'd died in the planning meeting and he should say a few words. He wrote back "they were a good coworker and they always had a pen." I do always have a pen. It's true.

Dinner was toast.
