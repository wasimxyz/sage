title: Hike with Tess
---

Tess dragged me up the ridge trail at eight in the morning and I didn't hate it. The first mile was the worst. After that my legs gave up complaining. At the top there's a rock where everybody takes the same photo, and we waited in line for it behind a couple who needed nine tries. We ate granola bars with a view while Tess told me about the guy she's seeing, who takes her bowling and has never once lost. I'm suspicious of anyone who has never lost.
