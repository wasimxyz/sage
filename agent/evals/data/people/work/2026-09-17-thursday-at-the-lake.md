title: Thursday at the lake
---

Slept until nine. Walked to the lake around noon. Nobody was there except a man feeding ducks and two women power-walking with arm weights. The light was low and gold even in the middle of the day. I sat on a bench for an hour and didn't take my phone out. I'd forgotten you could just sit on a bench. I'm not sure yet whether I like it, but I stayed.
