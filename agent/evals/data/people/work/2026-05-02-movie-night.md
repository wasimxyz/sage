Owen came over with a movie he said I'd love. It was about two sisters running a bakery in Finland, and nothing happens for ninety minutes and then someone makes a decision. I loved it, which annoyed me. Owen said he'd been right. He has a way of being right about movies that makes you want to disagree on principle.

We ordered way too much food and sat through the end credits like people at a church. He left at midnight and folded the blanket on the arm of the couch, which I took as a criticism.
