date: 2026-06-20
title: Rooftop barbecue
---

Tess's building has a roof deck with a grill, and she booked it for six o'clock. Eleven people. Owen brought a cooler and a speaker and played the same four songs for three hours. I made a potato salad that disappeared in twenty minutes, and a man I'd never met told me it was the best he'd had. I told him it was mostly mayonnaise. He said that's what makes it good.

Stayed until the sun went down and the string lights came on. Walked home with a paper plate of leftover corn.
