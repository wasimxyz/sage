title: Plants
---

Went to the nursery for one plant and left with six. A pothos, a snake plant, a thing the label called "forgiving," and three herbs I'll have forgotten to water by August. The woman at the register asked if I was starting a collection. I said no, and then I said maybe. The pothos is on the shelf above the couch. The couch and the pothos look good together. I did not plan that.
