title: Beach
---

Drove to the beach with Tess. We got there at ten and left at four, and I slept face down on the towel for most of it. I now have a perfect rectangle on my back where the sunscreen missed. The water was freezing and we stood in it up to our knees, announcing how it felt, until a kid ran straight past us and went all the way under. We both felt bad about that.
