title: River path
---

Rode the bike path along the river, about fourteen miles round trip. Hadn't done that since last summer. My legs are fine and my backside has filed a complaint. Stopped at the halfway point where a guy sells lemonade out of a cooler, with a very serious dog sitting next to him. The dog's name is Boss. I asked. The guy said Boss doesn't work for tips but will accept eye contact.

I got a little lost coming back, around where the path splits under the bridge, and ended up on a road with a view of the water treatment plant. That's fine. Everyone should see one.
