title: Elena's new place
---

Helped Elena move into her new apartment. It's on the fourth floor, the couch is eighty-four inches long, and the stairwell turns at forty-one inches. We did the math, then we did the other math, then we took the legs off, and it still took three of us and a neighbor who appeared out of nowhere and refused to leave until it was in. She gave him a beer and he drank it on the landing and left. I've never seen him before or since.

The place is nice. Big windows, a radiator that clanks like someone is hitting it with a wrench. We ate pad thai off the boxes on the floor because she can't find the forks. She says they're in a box marked KITCHEN, which is one of eleven boxes marked KITCHEN.

The building has a roof deck nobody uses and a laundry room in the basement that takes coins. She showed me both like a realtor. She has already named the radiator. It's Kevin.
