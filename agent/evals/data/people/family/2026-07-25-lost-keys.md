Lost my keys for two hours. Went through every pocket, the couch, the freezer for some reason. Elena said to retrace my steps, and my steps were: kitchen, bathroom, door. Found them in the pocket of the jacket I'd been wearing, which I had checked first. I know I did. I checked it first. They were in the other pocket. A pocket I didn't know that jacket had.
