title: Gutters
---

Dad cleaned his gutters today. He told me on the phone this morning, very casually, the way you'd mention a grocery list. The ladder is twenty-two feet and from 1998. I asked if someone could hold it for him. He said who. I said what about the neighbor. He said the neighbor has a knee.

So I spent the afternoon on the couch with my phone in my hand. What if he slips. What if he's on the ground behind the garage and nobody comes by until it's dark. I know how often that actually happens. I know. I called at two and he said "I'm on the ladder, call me back" and hung up, which I'm choosing to take as a good sign. Called again at four. He was done. He found a tennis ball up there, and a nest, and what he called "a lot of mulch," and he sounded pleased.

I asked him to call me if he goes up again. He said "yes, boss." He said I worry like him. He said it like a compliment. I'm not sure it is.
