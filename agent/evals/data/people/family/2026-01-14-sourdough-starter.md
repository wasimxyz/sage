title: The starter
---

Elena mailed me a jar of her sourdough starter, which is a strange thing to get in the mail. It arrived looking like grey wallpaper paste with a note that said FEED ME. I've named it Gerald. Followed the instructions: flour, water, a rubber band around the jar so I can see if it rises. By evening it had a few bubbles and I felt an irrational amount of joy about that.

If this works I am going to be insufferable about it.
