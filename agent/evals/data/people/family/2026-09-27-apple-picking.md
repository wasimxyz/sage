date: 2026-09-27
title: Apple picking
---

Drove out with Elena to pick apples, an hour each way, and we picked more than we could carry. Elena gets competitive about it. She counted hers. I stopped counting at thirty and started eating one. The farm gave us a bag the size of a pillow and a map nobody used. We argued about whether you twist or pull. She twists. I pull. We've been having this argument since we were kids and neither of us has ever once been persuaded.

Elena found a tree at the far end of the rows that nobody else had touched and made a speech about it. Then a bee flew into her sweatshirt and the speech ended. She was fine, it didn't sting, but she took the sweatshirt off in the middle of a field in front of a school group and put it back on inside out.

I'm now in possession of eleven pounds of apples and no plan for them. Pies, probably. Sauce. A lot of sauce.
