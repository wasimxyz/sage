title: Concert on the lawn
---

Outdoor concert tonight, lawn seats, a band I'd never heard of that Priya swore by. They were good. The singer talked between every song, mostly about their van. Someone behind us had a full folding table with a tablecloth and candles, which I regard as cheating. The mosquitoes held a meeting. I counted eleven bites on one leg.

We left before the encore to beat the parking lot, which was the right call and also a small sadness.
