title: Picnic
---

Longest day of the year, supposedly, so Priya and I went to the park at five and stayed until it got dark, which was after nine. We had bread, cheese, grapes, and a bottle of something fizzy that was warmer than we wanted. The ants found us in about four minutes. A man two blankets over was playing the ukulele, badly, and it got better the longer we sat there. I put a grape in my shoe by accident and didn't notice until I was home.

Walking back, Priya said summers used to last longer. I said they used to start when school ended. She said that's the same thing.
