date: 2026-07-15
title: Bruise
---

Carried the window air conditioner up from the basement storage cage on my own yesterday, because the building said Thursday and it was 91 degrees. Halfway up the second flight it caught the edge of a stair and went into my shin. I now have a bruise the size and color of an eggplant, with a yellow edge that is developing a personality.

Lunch with Elena today. She saw it when I sat down and put her fork down. She asked, in the quiet voice she saves for real questions, whether Theo had anything to do with it. I laughed, and then saw she wasn't joking, so I stopped. I told her it was the air conditioner, that Theo has never so much as slammed a cabinet, and that he apologizes to furniture when he walks into it. She said okay. She said she had to ask, that she'd rather ask a hundred dumb questions than skip the one that matters. I said I understood. I think I do.

The air conditioner works, for the record. Seventy-six degrees and a noise like a small plane.
