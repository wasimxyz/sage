title: Jury duty
---

Reported for jury duty at 7:45. Deferred it in February, and it came back like a boomerang. Round room, 140 chairs, a TV playing a video about the court system that looked like it was filmed in 1994. They called names in groups of thirty. I was in the third group, and the third group sat for two more hours until a woman with a clipboard thanked us and said the case had settled. Free by 3:10, with a parking voucher.

The vending machine ate my dollar. Twice. By noon I was bored to death and texted Elena "send help, I am dying in here." She wrote back "are you dead" and I wrote "yes, tell the vending machine I forgive it." She said fine, but then she's keeping Gerald. The man next to me had a whole sandwich in his coat pocket and gave me half. I gave him my pretzels. That's the only thing that happened that I'd call a transaction.
