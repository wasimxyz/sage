Called Mom and Dad on speaker and told them. Mom said "oh, honey." Dad said "well." Then Mom said, "Was it you or her?" I said it was her but also me. She said "Mm." She didn't say what she thought, which is worse. Dad asked if I was eating. I said yes. Mom said, "Come out Sunday."
