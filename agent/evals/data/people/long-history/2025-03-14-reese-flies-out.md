Drove Reese to the airport at 5 a.m. She hugged me at the curb with her backpack on and said she'd text when she landed. She did, a photo of mountains out the plane window. I sent back a heart. Went to Harlan and did my shift. Came home to an empty apartment and Noodle, who treated the quiet like a vacation. Ate over the sink and watched the whole of something I can't remember.
