title: Dad slipped
---

Dad slipped on the ice on the back steps and landed on his hip. He says he's fine. He's black and blue the size of a dinner plate. He wouldn't go to urgent care. Mom said, "You're going," and he said, "I'm going," and then he went. Nothing broken. Home by 3, complaining about the paperwork. Mom looked at me over his head and I knew what she was thinking. I don't like how much I think about the two of them at the top of those steps.
