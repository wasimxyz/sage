title: Eastway interview
---

Interview at Eastway. A big warehouse out past the airport, loading docks down the long side, a line of people already waiting at the side door at 10 a.m. for the Wednesday pantry. Denise, the director of operations, walked me through it herself. She's sixty or so, gray ponytail, running shoes, a clipboard she doesn't look at. She showed me the walk-in, a cooler the size of my old bedroom, and five vans with names on the doors like Gladys and The Brick. A volunteer in an orange vest was sorting cans by date and singing.

She asked what I'd change about how a warehouse runs. I said Harlan's on-time numbers are good because everybody's afraid of the slide. She laughed, which I didn't expect. She asked why I wanted to leave. I said I wanted to do something where I can see where it ends up. She didn't write that down. She said, "We have trucks nobody on the floor can see the end of, either." She said she'd call by Monday.
