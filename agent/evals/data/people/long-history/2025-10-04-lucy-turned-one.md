Lucy's first birthday party was today in Pittsburgh. I wasn't there. Mom had a neurologist appointment, Dad wanted me to drive, and I told Joelle on Wednesday. She said, "Of course, of course, we understand!" Sent a present, a wooden puzzle with big knobs, three days late. Joelle sent back a photo of Lucy with frosting on her face in a paper crown. I put it on the fridge. I owe Joelle a phone call. I've owed it since June.
