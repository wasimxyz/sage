title: Kiln accident
---

Three of my pots cracked in the kiln. A shelf shifted, Wendell said, a stilt came loose. The green bowls I was proudest of. Wendell was sorry in the way of someone who doesn't say it. He gave me a bag of clay for free. Nolan wanted to be angry at someone on my behalf and there was nobody. I said it's clay, it's fine. He said, "It's not fine, say it's not fine." I said, "It's not fine." I felt better for a minute.
