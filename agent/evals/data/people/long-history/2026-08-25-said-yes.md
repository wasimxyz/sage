Told the board yes. Called the chair first, because that's how it works. She said, "Good. Welcome." I'd expected a feeling and mostly it was lunch. Ate a sandwich in the office and then went out on the dock and told the drivers. One said, "We know." Another said, "Does this mean we get a raise?" I said no. Nobody was surprised.
