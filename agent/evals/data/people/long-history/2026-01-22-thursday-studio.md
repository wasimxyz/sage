Thursday open studio. I've started going every week. The guy at the next wheel makes vases so tall he has to stand to do the top. Long arms, gray sweater, glasses on a string. He pulled one up three feet and it wobbled and he caught it with a sigh like a kid falling off a bike. I watched too long. He caught me watching and said, "Don't look at it, it knows."
