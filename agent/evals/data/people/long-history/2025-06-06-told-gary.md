title: Told Gary
---

Told Gary in his office at 7:30, door shut. He leaned back in his chair and said, "Is this about money?" I said no. He said, "Because I'd have matched it." I said it's not the money. He said, "Then what?" and I said I wanted a change. That's all I said. I had a list in my head of maybe fifteen things. The Saturdays. The slide. Hearing about Buffalo from Hector's guy in payroll. I didn't say any of them. He said, "Well, I'm disappointed," and shook my hand, and I left.

In the hallway Hector gave me a thumbs up from across the dock before I'd said a word. Everybody knew.
