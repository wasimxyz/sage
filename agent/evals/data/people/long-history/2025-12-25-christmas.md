Christmas at the parents'. Mom wore the oatmeal cardigan. Dad wore a Santa hat for exactly one picture. Colin did video again and it's starting to feel like how we do things now. Dad cooked the whole meal himself, a ham, green beans, potatoes, Mom's rolls from a bag. He did well. He said, "Next year I'll do the stuffing from scratch." Mom said, "Not on your life," and he said, "Then from the box," and she said, "Now you're talking."

After dinner Mom and I sat on the sofa and she held my hand with the left one. It was warm.
