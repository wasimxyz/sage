Surgery at 8. Dad called at 11:40 from the waiting room and said "she's out, the doctor says it went fine, she's asking for juice." He sounded like he'd run a mile. I took a long lunch and ate in the car and called him back and let him tell it twice. Drove out after work. Mom was asleep with the leg up on a wedge and a thing on her foot that squeezes. Dad has been in the same chair since 5 a.m. I sent him home to eat and sat there until they kicked me out.
