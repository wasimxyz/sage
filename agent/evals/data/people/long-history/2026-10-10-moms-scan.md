title: Mom's scan
---

Mom's follow-up scan came back clear. Dad called at 9 a.m. and said it before I could say hello. "Clear. Clear. She's clear." I sat down on the porch steps. Mom got on the phone and said, "I told you." She had not told me. She hadn't told anybody. She'd been white-knuckled about this scan for six weeks and said nothing.
