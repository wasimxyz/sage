title: Cabbage
---

A pallet of cabbage arrived, nine hundred pounds, from a farm that overplanted. Nobody knew what to do with it. Denise said, "Put it on the dock and call it St. Patrick's." It was gone by Friday. A woman at the pantry told me her grandmother's recipe and I wrote it on a sticky note and I'm going to make it.
