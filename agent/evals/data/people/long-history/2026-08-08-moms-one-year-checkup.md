title: Mom's one-year checkup
---

Mom's one-year checkup, the scan and the blood work. The neurologist said her recovery is "better than average" and wants one more scan in October, just to be sure. He handed her a card for a driving evaluation. Dad wrote down everything. Mom asked if she could have the card framed. In the car she said, "I don't want to be better than average. I want to be what I was." I said I know. She said, "I know you know."
