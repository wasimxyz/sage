Mom's birthday. Called at lunch and she was in a good mood, knee and all. Sent her a cardigan the color of oatmeal that she'd looked at in a catalog. She said it's too nice to wear and she's wearing it right now. Dad got on the phone and said happy birthday to her through me, even though she was right there.
