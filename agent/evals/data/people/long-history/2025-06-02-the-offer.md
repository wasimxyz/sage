title: The offer
---

Denise called at 4:10 while I was in the loading dock office with the door open. I took it in the stairwell. She said, "We'd like to offer you the job," and then the number, which is what the posting said. She said she'd like me to start July 7th if I can give notice. I said I'd call her Wednesday. I stood in the stairwell for a minute. Somebody went past with a hand truck and said, "You good?" I said yes. I'm not sure what I am.
