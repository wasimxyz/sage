Reese asked tonight, calmly, over dishes, "Have you thought about it?" I said I think about it all the time. She said "And?" I said I'm still thinking. She nodded and dried one plate for a long time. She has to tell the school by the 28th. I said I know. I said I'm not ready to answer. She said "I know you're not." I'm not sure what that meant. I'm not sure what I want it to mean.
