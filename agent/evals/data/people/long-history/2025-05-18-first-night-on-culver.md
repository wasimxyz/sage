title: First night on Culver
---

First night was loud in a different way. The fridge hums on a note I can feel in my teeth, and the floor creaks every time Hal walks to his kitchen, which is often. Pepper barks twice at midnight and twice at six. Noodle slept at the foot of the bed like she's always lived here. I woke at 3 and didn't know where the bathroom was.
