title: Pittsburgh
---

Drove to Pittsburgh to meet Lucy. Four hours, one stop for gas and a bad coffee. Joelle opened the door in a sweatshirt with spit-up on the shoulder and hugged me for a long time and said she hadn't showered since Thursday. Lucy is nine weeks old and has a lot of opinions. I held her for an hour. She fell asleep on me and I didn't move, even when my arm went numb.

Joelle and I talked in the kitchen while her husband napped upstairs. She asked about Reese and work and I said fine, fine. She said you always say fine. I said it's true. We ate takeout dumplings on the floor like we used to in the dorm. I told her I'd come back in the spring.
