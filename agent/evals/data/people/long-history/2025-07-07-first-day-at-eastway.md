title: First day at Eastway
---

First day at Eastway. Parked across from the docks at 7:30 and a woman in a bright green sweatshirt waved me over and said, "You must be the new one." Imani. She started three weeks ago, does donor logistics, and is on her second sweatshirt of the week. She walked me through everything: where the badge reader fails, the one good bathroom, how to get the walk-in door to latch. Denise handed me a laminated map and told me to ignore it.

Spent the morning with the vans and the afternoon with a grocery chain's spreadsheet of expiring dairy. At 3 a pallet of yogurt turned out to be four days past date and everyone just sighed and started on it. Nobody made a slide. Went home with a sore back and hands that smelled like cardboard, and I was happy.
