date: 2026-10-03
title: Coffee with Joelle
---

Coffee with Joelle on Monroe. She was already there at the table by the window with Lucy in a high chair eating the inside of a croissant. Lucy is two now, with a lot of hair and a vocabulary of about eleven words, three of which were "no." Joelle looked older and exactly the same. She hugged me and said, "You look great," and I said, "You too."

We talked for an hour and ten minutes. The wedding, her husband's job, Lucy's sleep, my job, Nolan, the food bank. We talked about everything except last August and Lucy's birthday and the winter. Once she said, "I'm sorry I've been so," and stopped, and said, "busy." I said, "Me too." We both looked at Lucy. Lucy threw a piece of croissant at me.

Walked her to her car. She said, "Let's not take so long." I said, "Let's not." Then I went to the studio and threw cups until it got dark.
