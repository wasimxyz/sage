title: All-hands
---

All-hands at 2. Denise had an announcement. She said it standing at the front of the break room with a clipboard she wasn't looking at, which is how I knew it was serious. She's retiring at the end of June. Thirty-one years in food service, eleven here. She said, "I'd like to tell you it's a surprise, but I told the board in January." People clapped. Then people didn't know what to do and clapped again. A volunteer at the back cried.

Afterward she came to my desk and said, "You should think about it." She didn't say what "it" was. She went back to her office. I sat there pretending to look at a spreadsheet.
