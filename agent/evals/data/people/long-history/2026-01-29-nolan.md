The tall vase guy's name is Nolan. He lent me his trimming loop when mine went missing, then asked if I wanted a coffee from the machine in the lobby, which is not coffee. We drank it standing at the shelves. He works at the county library, in reference. He answers questions about tax forms and where the bathrooms are and whether a certain bird is a hawk. He said, "Mostly it's the bathrooms."
