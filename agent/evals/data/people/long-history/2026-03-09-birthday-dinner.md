title: Birthday dinner
---

Thirty-six. Dinner at the ramen place on Park with Imani and Nolan. Imani made the reservation and told them it was a birthday, so they brought out a scoop of ice cream with a candle in it and the whole staff sang. The tables on both sides joined in. Nolan sang harmony. Imani gave me a plant she says can't be killed. Nolan gave me a book of bird drawings he found at the library sale. I gave nobody anything and nobody minded.

We split a plate of dumplings and talked about the studio and her new job and a man who keeps calling the library to ask about a book that doesn't exist. I watched the candle burn down on the ice cream and then blew it out. It was a good dinner.
