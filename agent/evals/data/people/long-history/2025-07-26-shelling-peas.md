title: Shelling peas
---

Drove out to Batavia. Mom looks fine, honestly. Sat on the porch with her and shelled peas for twenty minutes, her fingers slower than I remember. She said she's tired "because it's summer." Dad hovered. I told them both to call the doctor and Mom said "I'm sixty-eight" as if that explained it. Dad said they'd call Monday.
