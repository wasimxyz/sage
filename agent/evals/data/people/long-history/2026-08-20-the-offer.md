title: The offer

The board called me in at 3. Four of them on speaker and one in the room, the chair, back from Maine. She said they want me to be the permanent warehouse and logistics manager and gave the number, which is Denise's minus a few thousand because I'm new. I'd have said yes in the room. She said, "You don't have to answer today." I said I'd answer by Tuesday. Then I sat in the office for a long time. I wasn't scared. I wasn't anything. Looked at the clipboard in the drawer. Called nobody.
