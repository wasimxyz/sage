title: Thanksgiving at Mom and Dad's
---

Drove out to Batavia with the pie. Dad carved, same as every year, with the electric knife he got in 1998 and treats like a family member. Mom sat in the good chair with a bag of frozen peas on her right knee and gave instructions from there. Her surgery is January 14th. She said she's looking forward to it the way you look forward to the dentist.

Reese talked to Dad about the Bills for twenty minutes, which she can do with no knowledge of football, and I admire it. Colin called from Portland and they put him on speaker at the table. He ate his dinner at 11 a.m. his time and told us about it. Mom made the stuffing from the box and the stuffing from scratch and said nobody was allowed to say which one was better. It's the box one. Home by 8. Reese drove.
