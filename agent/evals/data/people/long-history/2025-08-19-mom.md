date: 2025-08-19
title: Mom
---

Dad called at 7:10. I was in the shower and let it ring and then he called again. He said, "Your mother can't, her face is wrong, they're coming." That's all he said and I could hear her in the background, a sound, not words. I told him to stay on the line and I got in the car with wet hair and no socks. He had a 911 operator on the other phone telling him things. Then the ambulance came and the line went to noise.

It's forty minutes to Batavia. I did it in thirty. The whole way I kept thinking what if she's already gone by the time I get there. I said it out loud in the car. What if she's gone, what if she's gone. I don't know why. I think I wanted to hear how it sounded so I'd be ready.

She wasn't gone. She was in a bed in the ER with a monitor, awake, the left side of her face slack and her left hand curled on the blanket. She looked at me and her mouth tried to say something and what came out was soft and wrong. Dad was at the bed holding her good hand with both of his. A doctor said stroke, caught early, that they'd given her something to break up the clot and would know more in a few hours. Dad said, "It was right there at breakfast. She was holding the coffee pot."

I stayed. Called Denise from the parking lot and she said, "Go. Don't call in. Don't think about it." Called Colin and it went to voicemail and I said it plainly, which was harder than I expected. My eyes hurt. I'm writing this in the waiting room on my phone.
