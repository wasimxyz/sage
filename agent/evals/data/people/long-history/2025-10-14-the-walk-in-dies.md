date: 2025-10-14
title: The walk-in dies
---

The compressor on the walk-in quit at 6 a.m. By 8 we were moving four thousand pounds of dairy into a loaner truck from the grocery chain, the whole crew passing crates hand to hand. Forty-one degrees in the cooler by 9, climbing. Imani and I did the last shelf together. I said, "This inventory is going to kill me." She said, "If you go in there I'll tell Denise the count was off by twelve cases." I said put it on the stone. "Here lies the coordinator. The yogurt was fine." We laughed until we had to put the crates down.

Denise heard from the doorway and said, "Nobody is dying on my shift. Twelve cases of cream cheese would be a loss, though." We lost nothing. The repair guy came at 3 and said the part was fine, it was a breaker.
