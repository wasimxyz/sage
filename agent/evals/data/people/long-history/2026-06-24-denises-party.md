title: Denise's party
---

Denise's retirement party in the warehouse. A banner and a sheet cake the size of a door. Forty volunteers, three drivers, two of the board. She cried once and said so. She gave a speech about how nobody at a food bank wants to be thanked, they want a bag of groceries and no questions. Then she thanked everybody anyway.

At the end she pulled me aside and said, "The board called me this morning. They want you to run it for the summer and decide in August. Acting. Don't say I told you." I said nothing. She said, "Say something." I said, "Thank you." She said, "Oh, for heaven's sake."
