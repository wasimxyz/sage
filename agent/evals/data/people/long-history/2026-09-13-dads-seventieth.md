title: Dad's seventieth
---

Dad's seventieth. Colin flew in. Mom did the whole thing herself: a cake, four tables in the backyard, folding chairs borrowed from the church, forty people. She stood to give the toast with the cane hooked on the table and said, "I almost missed this one." Everybody got quiet. She said, "But I didn't," and Dad stood up so fast his chair fell over.

Nolan came for the afternoon. I hadn't planned it and then it was a thing. He drove himself because I said I wasn't sure. Mom said, "He's tall." Dad asked what he does. Nolan said, "Reference librarian," and Dad said that sounds like steady work. Nolan said it's mostly bathrooms and Dad laughed the way Hal did.

After, I did dishes with Dad. He said, "We couldn't have done all the driving without you." I said sure. He said, "I mean it." He dried one plate for a long time.
