Easter. Mom came down the porch steps by herself, one hand on the rail, no cane. Dad pretended not to watch and then watched the whole thing. Ham again, and deviled eggs on Mom's mother's dish. Colin FaceTimed during dessert and Mom held the phone like a plate.

I told them about Harlan folding into Buffalo. Mom said, "Well, you could move to Buffalo. It's only an hour." I said it's an hour and ten. She said "Hmm."
