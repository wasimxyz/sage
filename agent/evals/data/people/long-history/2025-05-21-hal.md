Hal brought up a tomato plant in a coffee can and said "I start too many." He told me about his route, thirty-one years, and the dog that bit him in 1996. He asked what I do and I said dispatch. He said, "Good, somebody has to know where everything is." I put the plant on the porch. I'll probably kill it.
