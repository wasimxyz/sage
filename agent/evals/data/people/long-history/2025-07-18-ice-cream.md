title: Ice cream
---

Another truck, another date problem. This one was ice cream, hours past, a trailer's worth. Denise stood at the dock with her arms crossed and said, "What would you do?" I said call the grocery's logistics lead and ask what they'd do. She said, "Good, call." I called. Nobody had a plan. We threw it out and Denise wrote the number on a sticky note and put it on the wall in the break room like a trophy.
