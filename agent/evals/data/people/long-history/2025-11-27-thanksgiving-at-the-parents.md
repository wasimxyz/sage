date: 2025-11-27
title: Thanksgiving at the parents'
---

Drove out to Batavia at 10 with the pie and a bag of rolls. Dad carved again with the electric knife, except this year he held it in both hands like a man defusing something. Mom sat at the table, not in the good chair, with the walker parked behind her and her left hand in her lap. She said she wanted to be at the table with everyone. She cut her own turkey with a rocker knife Dad bought from a catalog. It took a while and nobody said anything.

Colin's face was on the iPad propped against the gravy boat. He started to say grace by accident because he thought we'd begun. Mom made the stuffing from scratch and the box one too, from her chair, with Dad as her hands, and I asked which was better and she said, "You know which." I do. It's the box one.

Dad said a few words about how glad he is that we're all here, and stopped, and said, "That's all." Home by 7.
