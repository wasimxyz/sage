date: 2025-09-12
title: Mom comes home
---

Mom came home today with a walker and a binder of instructions as thick as a phone book. The discharge nurse said she's done better than they hoped. Dad put grab bars in the bathroom himself, by drill and by YouTube, and one is at a slight angle that Mom says gives it character. The house smelled like the soup he made, scorched a little at the bottom. We ate it anyway.

Mom said the left hand still isn't hers and she wants to use it for the spoon. She spilled twice and wouldn't take help. She slept in her own bed and Dad slept in the chair next to it.
