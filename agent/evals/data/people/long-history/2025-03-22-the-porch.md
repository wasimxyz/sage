---
date: 2025-03-22
title: The porch
---

Reese and I talked on the front porch tonight. She asked me to come out. It was cold, thirty-something, and she'd brought out two blankets, which is the kind of thing she does. She'd been practicing, I could tell. She said she's been waiting ten weeks for me to tell her yes or no and I haven't said either one. She said she's going to Denver in August and she can't spend five months guessing. "I'm taking your silence as my answer," she said. "And I think it is the answer. I think you know it too."

I said that's not fair. She said, "Then tell me I'm wrong." And I sat there. I couldn't say anything, and the longer I didn't, the more it was true. She cried a little and I didn't know where to put my hands. She said she isn't angry. She said she'd rather have a clean end than a slow one.

She's staying at her friend's tonight. I came in and sat on the couch under the blanket and Noodle came and sat on my feet. I'm not going to write more tonight.
