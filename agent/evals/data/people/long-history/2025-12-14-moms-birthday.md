date: 2025-12-14
title: Mom's birthday
---

Mom's sixty-ninth. Drove out with the bowls. She unwrapped the first with her right hand, held it up, and didn't say anything, and then said, "You made this?" I said I did. She turned it over and looked at the bottom and said, "You put your initials." She asked for soup in it. Dad made soup from a can and Mom ate every bite out of the bowl with her left hand on the rim. She said the green is the color of the lake in September. It isn't, but I let her.
