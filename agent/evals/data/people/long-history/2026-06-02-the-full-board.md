Second interview, with the full board. Eight people around a long table and one on a speakerphone. They asked about the budget, about the union at the county, about what I'd do with a truck that breaks down on a Saturday. I said I'd call a driver and I know whose number to call. A man in the corner said, "Good answer." I said it's the true answer. Denise was out in the lobby on the phone with her hand over her mouth when I came out.
