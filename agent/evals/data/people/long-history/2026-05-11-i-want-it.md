title: I want it
---

First interview for Denise's job, in the conference room at Eastway at 10, with Denise and two board members. The posting went up on the 1st and I applied the same day. I'd rehearsed one sentence. One of the board members asked why I wanted it and I said it right away, before I could change it. "I want it." I said I'd been here ten months and from my desk I can see how it ends and I'd like to be the one who decides. Nobody said anything for a second. Then the other board member asked about the vans.

I walked out and sat in the Fit with my hands shaking. I texted Nolan, which surprised me. "I think I said what I wanted." He wrote back, "Then good." That's all.
