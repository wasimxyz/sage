title: First wheel class
---

First class at Clay Works. Eight of us, a man called Wendell who has hands like oven mitts, and a lot of wet gray clay. We spent the first half hour wedging it, which is kneading, which I'm good at because I've made bread. Then he put us on the wheel and said "center it." It will not center. It went off to the left. It went off to the right. At one point it left the wheel completely and hit the woman next to me, who laughed. I have clay in my hair and under every fingernail. I want to go back.
