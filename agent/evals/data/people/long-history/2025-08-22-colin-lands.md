title: Colin lands

Colin landed at 3 and I picked him up at the airport. He hadn't slept, he'd been up since the call. He looks like Dad when he's worried. We drove straight to Strong. He walked into Mom's room and stopped in the door. She said "Colin" very clearly and he started crying in the doorway and Mom said, "Oh, stop," and everybody laughed.
