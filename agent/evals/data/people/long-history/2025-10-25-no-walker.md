date: 2025-10-25
title: No walker
---

Mom walked from the kitchen to the living room with no walker. Fourteen steps. Dad counted out loud. I was in the doorway with a plate of apple cake and I stopped chewing. She sat down in the recliner, out of breath, and said, "That's one." Dad had to go into the other room for a minute and make noise with a cabinet.
