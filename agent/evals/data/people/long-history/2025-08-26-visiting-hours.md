Strong, after work, again. Mom does more physical therapy than I've done in my life. She was wearing the oatmeal cardigan over the hospital gown. Dad said she asked for it. She can lift her left hand to her shoulder now. She told me she can't think of the word for the thing you eat with and held up a fork. We said it over and over. "Fork." She got it on the ninth try and threw a napkin at me.
