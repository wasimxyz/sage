Clay Works holiday sale. Wendell let the students put a few things out. I put out four mugs and a small dish. A woman in a long red coat bought one of the mugs, $14, and asked if I made it. I said yes, with my hands. She said, "I can tell." Not sure how to take that. Imani bought the dish and told the whole studio I was famous.
