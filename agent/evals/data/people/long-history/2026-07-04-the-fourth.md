title: The Fourth
---

Fireworks at the lake with Nolan, on a blanket from his trunk. Kids with sparklers, a smell like pennies. During the finale he said, "Hey, are we doing this?" and I said I thought we already were. He said, "I wanted it on the record." I said, "On the record." He wrote it on my arm in pen like we were in sixth grade. I wore a sweatshirt the rest of the night so Hal wouldn't see.
