All-hands at 2. Gary had a slide with the word "transition" on it. The Rochester dispatch desk is being folded into the Buffalo hub by the end of the summer. Four of the seven of us get offered jobs there, an hour and ten minutes away on a good day, or a package. He said "nobody here is being let go" and then said "we'll be scheduling one-on-ones." I'm one of the four. Hector is one of the four.

Hector put his head in his hands after, in the stairwell, where he thought nobody would see. I stood next to him and didn't say anything. Then we went back up and finished the afternoon dispatch. I sat in the Fit in the lot for a long time before I started it.
