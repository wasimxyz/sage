title: Culver Road
---

Looked at a place on Culver Road after work. Second floor of a duplex, one bedroom, a porch the size of a tablecloth. $975. The downstairs neighbor, Hal, came out in slippers with a beagle and said "You'll want to know the floors creak." He's retired from the post office. Pepper smelled my shoes for a full minute. The landlord is Hal's cousin.

Hal said the heat in the building is old but quiet. I'll find out in November. I said I'd take it.
