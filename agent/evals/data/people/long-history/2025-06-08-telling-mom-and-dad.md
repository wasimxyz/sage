title: Telling Mom and Dad
---

Drove to Batavia to tell Mom and Dad in person. Mom said "a food bank?" and then "Well, that's something to be proud of." Dad asked about the benefits, which is his whole personality. Mom said I looked tired. She walked me out to the car with no cane and stood at the end of the driveway until I turned the corner. In the mirror she looked small.
