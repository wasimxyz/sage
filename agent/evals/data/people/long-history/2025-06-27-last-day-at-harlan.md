title: Last day at Harlan
---

Last day. Cake in the break room, the grocery store kind, again. Someone signed a card "Good luck on your next adventure!" with a drawing of a truck. Gary came by at 2, shook my hand again, and said, "You know where we are." I said thanks for everything. I meant about half of it. Hector walked me out to the Fit and stood there with his hands in his jacket and said, "I'll text you the second I find out who they put in your chair." I laughed, got in the car, and didn't cry until Lake Avenue.
