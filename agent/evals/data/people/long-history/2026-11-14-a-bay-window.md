title: A bay window
---

Saturday. Hal raking. Noodle on the sill. I made a list of what I like about my place and a list of what I like about Nolan's place and a list of what two bedrooms and a studio fee would cost. The lists are the same length. It's 4 p.m. and the light is already going. Nolan texted a photo of a place he found online, a brick building with a bay window. "No pressure," he wrote. I said, "Looks nice." I haven't written back since.
