title: Imani's news
---

Imani got an offer from the county's food assistance office. A coordinator job, better money, regular hours. She told me in the cooler, where nobody could hear. She said, "I haven't said yes. I wanted you to know first." I said that's great. I said you should take it. She looked at me for a second like she was waiting for the next part and then said, "Okay."
