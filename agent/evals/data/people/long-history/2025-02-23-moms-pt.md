Drove out for Mom's physical therapy because Dad doesn't like the parking garage. The therapist had her do stairs, four steps with a rail, up and down. She did it with her jaw set. Dad stood in the corner with his hands in his pockets and mouthed "good" at her. On the way home she said she's tired of being the patient. I said you're doing great and she said, "That's what you say to the patient."
