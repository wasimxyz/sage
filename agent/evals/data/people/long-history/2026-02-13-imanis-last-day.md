title: Imani's last day
---

Imani's last day. Cake, the grocery store kind. Denise gave a short speech and called her "the heart of the dock," which Imani said she wants on her gravestone. We all signed a card. Mine says "Good luck. You'll be great." I wrote it twice. She hugged me in the parking lot and said, "Thursday, right? Studio?" I said sure. She drove off in the little car with the hat with ears on the dash. I sat in the Fit for ten minutes. I had a lot to say, some of it about August, and none of it came out.
