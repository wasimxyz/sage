title: Moving day
---

Moved. Hector came at 8 with his brother-in-law's pickup and a case of water. We did the whole thing in three trips. The couch is new, from the thrift place on Lyell, a green corduroy thing that two of us carried up the stairs while Hal stood at the bottom saying "tilt it" to nobody.

Reese stopped by at 11 with a box that had the rest of my mugs in it and the pan she'd decided I should keep after all. She didn't come in. She looked up the stairs and said, "It looks nice." I said it's small. She said, "Small is okay." Then she left. Hector pretended to be very interested in a lamp.

Noodle spent the whole afternoon behind the bathroom door making the noise she makes. By dark she was out and sitting on the bedroom sill looking at the street. Hector left at 6 and I paid him in pizza. Sat on the green couch in a room full of boxes and didn't open any of them.
