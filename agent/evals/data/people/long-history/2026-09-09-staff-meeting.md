First staff meeting I ran. Eleven people in the break room, a table of donuts, no slides. I read from the clipboard, the way Denise did, except I actually looked at it. A driver asked about overtime and I said I don't know yet, I'll find out, and I wrote it down. Walking out, one of them said, "That was good." A volunteer said, "Short." I said it was supposed to be.
