title: Kiln opening
---

Kiln opening at Clay Works. Wendell cracked the lid at 4 and the whole class stood around waiting like it was a birth. My glaze came out right for once, a pale green with a blue edge, six small bowls. One of Nolan's vases cracked straight down the side. He said "It's fine" in a voice that meant it wasn't. I said I'd keep the cracked half on my shelf. He said, "You don't have to." I said, "I want to." He looked at me for a while. Then he kissed me, quickly, in front of the kiln, the way you do something before you can change your mind.

Wendell said, "Well, that's one way to open a kiln." Nobody clapped, thank God. I walked home. Noodle was on the porch and looked at me and I looked back and neither of us said anything.
