title: Mom drove
---

Mom drove. A hundred yards, to the end of the street and back, in the Buick, with Dad in the passenger seat gripping the door handle. I stood on the porch and watched the car go down the street, slow, signal on, at a crawl. She parked it perfectly in the driveway. She got out and said, "There." Dad's hands were still shaking. Mom's weren't.
