title: Church group

A church group of forty came to sort. They showed up in matching shirts and spent two hours debating the best way to stack canned corn. Imani ran the floor like a drill sergeant who loves everybody. I stood with Denise in the doorway while she said, "Look at that. Forty people who had nothing to do on a Tuesday." She doesn't smile much and she was smiling.
