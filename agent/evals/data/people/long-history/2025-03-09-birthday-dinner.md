date: 2025-03-09
title: Birthday dinner
---

Thirty-five. Reese took me to Sorrento's on Monroe, the place with the red booths where we had our first real dinner. She'd made the reservation weeks ago. Candle in the tiramisu, the waiter sang, and the table behind us sang along, badly. Reese gave me a book of essays and a really good pen. I gave her nothing, because she told me not to, and I should have anyway.

We split the tiramisu and talked about her mother's garden and the weather. She held my hand across the table at one point and I let her. It was a good dinner. I kept watching the candle burn down.
