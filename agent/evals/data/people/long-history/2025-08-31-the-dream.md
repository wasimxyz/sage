title: The dream
---

Dreamed Dad died. Not in a sad way, in a paperwork way. I was in his closet picking out a tie because the funeral home had called, the viewing was at two, and I only had a blue tie and a green one. The more I looked the uglier all of Dad's ties got and I was furious at him for it. In the dream I said, "He should have bought better ties." Woke at 4:30 with my heart going.

Dad is fine. He's at Mom's rehab by 8 every day with a thermos. I called him at 7 just to hear him say hello and he said, "What's wrong?" and I said nothing, I wanted to hear you pick up.
