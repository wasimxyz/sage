title: Hard talk
---

First hard conversation as manager. A driver who's been late four times in a month. I scheduled it for 7:30 in the office with the door shut. I'd practiced. I said, "I'm worried about the pattern," and he said, "I know," and we talked about his daughter's school bus and his sister's shifts. We rearranged his route. He said, "You're not what I expected." I said, "What did you expect?" He said, "Somebody with a slide."
