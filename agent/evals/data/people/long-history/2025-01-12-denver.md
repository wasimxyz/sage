title: Denver
---

Reese got the Denver job. The offer came Friday and she waited until today to tell me, which I understand. Chemistry, a bigger school, more money than she makes now, starts in August. Her sister can find her a sublet near the school. She told me all of it at the kitchen table with the printed letter, like she'd rehearsed. Then she said, "I want you to come. I want to be clear that I'm asking."

I said that's a huge decision. I said I need to think about Mom's surgery, and work, and Noodle, and the lease. All true. She said, "Take your time. I have until the end of February to tell them."

I went for a walk after. It was 9 degrees. I walked to the river and back and I didn't have an answer when I got home either. I keep thinking that if I don't say anything for a bit it'll get clearer.
