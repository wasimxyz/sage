title: The hallway

Dreamed about the hallway at the old house. In the dream I couldn't find my shoes and knew I needed them. That was the whole dream. Shoes, hallway, dark.

Woke up at four. My place, my door, bolt turned. Got up and checked it anyway. Slept again around six.

Just a dream. Third one since summer.
