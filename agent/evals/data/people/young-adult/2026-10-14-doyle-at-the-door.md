date: 2026-10-14
title: Doyle at the door
---

Doyle came at seven this morning. No text first. He pounded on the door and I went stiff before I knew it was him. Back against the wall, breathing wrong. Then I heard his voice. "Checking the radiator." That's all it was.

Loud knocking at home meant something else once. My body hasn't caught up. That's old. It isn't about him. He's a man with a clipboard.

He came in without asking, bled the radiator, stood there with his arms crossed, and said it's fine. It's not fine. It was cold when he left. I'd have said no if he'd asked.

Stood by the window afterward until my hands quit.
