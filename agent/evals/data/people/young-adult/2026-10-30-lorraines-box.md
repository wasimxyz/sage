title: Lorraine's box
---

Box from Aunt Lorraine. Wool socks, three pairs. A jar of her pickles. A card with a twenty in it that she thinks I won't find. I found it.

She wrote Come for Thanksgiving and underlined it.

Thanksgiving at hers was the one day that was always fine. Every year, even the bad ones. Turkey, her neighbor's dog under the table, nobody yelling.

The bus to her place is ninety minutes. I work till three that day, so maybe the evening bus. Haven't decided.
