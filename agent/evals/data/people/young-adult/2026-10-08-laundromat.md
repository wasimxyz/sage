title: Laundromat
---

Laundromat. Four dollars a load, two loads. Read the whole sign about what not to put in the dryer while I waited. Nothing on it applied to me. Folded the sheets twice.

A woman with a toddler needed quarters. I had them. Gave her four. She said thank you three times and I wanted to leave.
