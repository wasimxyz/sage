title: Aunt Lorraine
---

Aunt Lorraine called. Twelve minutes. Twenty-two years old and she still asks if I'm eating. Said yes. She asked about Labor Day. Said maybe, which means no, and she knows it.

I lived at her place on and off from nine to seventeen. The spare room had a shelf that was mine. Things at home were bad. Her place wasn't. That's all I'm going to say about that.

Mom left when I was little. Nothing to add there either.

Lorraine calls once a month. Hasn't missed one.
