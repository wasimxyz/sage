title: Double booked
---

The auditorium got double booked this morning. Two departments, same slot, both with guest speakers coming in. It was my mistake. I had put one of them on the wrong day, a Tuesday instead of a Thursday, and I didn't catch it until a woman showed up at the door asking where her people were.

I spent forty minutes on the phone and got one group moved to the library annex, which has a projector that works if you jiggle the cable. Both speakers went on. Nobody yelled. My manager said "these things happen" and then asked me to add a second check to the booking process, which is fair.

I still feel a bit sick about it. I keep replaying the moment I typed the wrong date. Dev bought me a coffee and said everybody does it once. I said I'd rather not be everybody.
