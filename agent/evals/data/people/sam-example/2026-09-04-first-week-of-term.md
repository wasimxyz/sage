title: First week of term
---

Classes started Tuesday and the office has been a zoo. Every professor wants the same four rooms at the same hour and I'm the person they email. By Wednesday afternoon I had eleven unread messages that began with "Quick question." None of them were quick.

I came up with a system. Anything about a room goes into the spreadsheet first, then I reply, so I stop answering from memory. It helped. Dev saw me doing it and said I was turning into a bureaucrat, and I said I'd always been one, I just hadn't had enough forms before.

The worst one was a lecturer who wanted the big lecture hall for a class of nine. I said no, politely, and offered the seminar room with the good windows. He said he'd think about it. By Thursday morning he'd taken it.

Ate cereal for dinner standing up tonight, too tired to cook. Tuesday I'm bringing lunch so I don't end up at the vending machine again.
