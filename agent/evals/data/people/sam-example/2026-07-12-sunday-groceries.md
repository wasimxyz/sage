title: Sunday groceries
---

Did the big grocery run this morning before it got hot. I forgot the list on the counter and bought most of it from memory, so now I own three jars of mustard. Sam came over for dinner and brought tomatoes from the stall by his building. We ate them with salt and bread and nothing else, standing at the counter. That was the whole dinner and it was better than anything I would have cooked.

He fell asleep on the couch around nine with the fan pointed right at him. I did the dishes and left him there for a while.
