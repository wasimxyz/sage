title: Barbecue
---

Sam's coworker Greg had a barbecue at his house and I went with Sam. I didn't know anyone. Sam kept getting pulled into conversations about a client I'd never heard of, so I stood next to the cooler for about an hour holding a lemonade I didn't want.

Eventually a woman who I think was Greg's wife's cousin asked if I wanted to see the garden. It turned into a long talk about tomatoes and a shed she's building by herself. She had a lot of opinions about lumber. I asked questions and she seemed happy about it. That was the best part of the afternoon.

The food was really good. Greg does something to corn that I need to find out about. I had two helpings and a brownie from a tray that was somehow still cold.

On the drive home Sam asked if I'd had an okay time and I said yes, which was mostly true. He said I looked like I was waiting for a bus. I said I kind of was. He laughed and said next time we'd leave whenever I wanted. That was all I needed to hear, and I don't know why I couldn't just say so at the time.
