title: The shelf
---

Put up the shelf over the desk today. It took two hours and four trips to the hardware store because I bought the wrong anchors, and then the wrong drill bit. The man at the counter finally walked me down the aisle and put the right box in my hand. I think he felt bad for me.

The shelf is crooked. Not by a lot, maybe a quarter inch over its whole length. I stood there with the level for a while and decided I would rather have a crooked shelf than do it again. The books are up. Nothing slid off, so I'm calling it done.
