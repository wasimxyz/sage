date: 2026-08-09
title: Quiet again
---

Took Lena to the bus station at seven this morning. She hugged me for a long time and told me to eat vegetables, like I'm twelve. I said I would.

Came home and the apartment was too quiet for about an hour. I stripped her bed and found one of her earrings under the nightstand, and I put it on the windowsill so I'd remember to mail it back. Then I just sat there for a while. It's strange how fast a place goes back to normal. By noon it was like she'd never been here, except for the earring.
