Sam came over with two pumpkins and a kitchen knife that was too big for the job. We covered the table in newspaper and I carved a face that was supposed to be a cat and came out looking like a surprised potato. His is better. He used a stencil and pretended he hadn't.

We still don't have costumes for tomorrow's party at his friend's place. He wants to go as a pair of salt and pepper shakers. I said we would need actual shakers. We settled on wearing black and white and calling it a theme. I'll probably regret that in a room full of people in real costumes.
