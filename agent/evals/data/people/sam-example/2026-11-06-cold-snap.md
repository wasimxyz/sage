title: Cold snap

First properly cold day. There was frost on the windows when I woke up and the radiator was clanking like someone was inside it. I found my boots in the back of the closet and they still fit. I walked to work in them and my toes were fine for once.

Dev came in with a scarf wrapped about six times around his neck and refused to take it off until noon. It made the office both chilly and funny. Tonight I'm making tea and going to bed at nine like an old person.
