title: Lena is here
---

Lena got in at noon. I took the afternoon off, which Dev thought was hilarious because I never take time off. We got her bag up the stairs and she did a lap of the apartment and said it was smaller than she pictured but nice. That's about right for her.

We walked to the corner place for dinner and she asked me a hundred questions about work. I kept answering and she kept saying "huh," like she was trying to find the interesting part. I don't blame her. I'd have the same trouble.

Sam met us for dessert. I was nervous the whole walk over and it was fine. He asked her about the library where she works and she told him about the man who returns the same mystery novel every single week, and they were off. I mostly watched. I had forgotten she's good at this, getting people to talk.

She's asleep in my bed now. I'm on the couch and the bar is exactly where I remembered it.
