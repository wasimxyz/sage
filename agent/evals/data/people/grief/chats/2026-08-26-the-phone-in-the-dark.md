title: The phone in the dark
---
User: I keep thinking about that dream from Monday. The hospital calling about Claire.
Sage: How is it sitting with you now, a couple of days later?
User: Better. Claire is fine. She's boring, she says. But the dream didn't come from nowhere. The real call about Mom came on a Sunday at 5:40 in the morning. It was Claire on the line, not the hospital. She said, "You should come now."
Sage: So a phone ringing in the dark has been waiting in you since then.
User: Yeah. I've never told her I think about it. Seventeen months and I've never said it.
Sage: Do you think she'd want to know?
User: Probably. She has her own version of that morning. I think we've been protecting each other.
Sage: Protecting each other is a kind of love, even when it leaves each of you alone with the same memory. You don't have to decide anything today.
User: I might tell her next time she visits. Not over the phone.
Sage: That sounds right. In person, with time, and no phone in the room.
