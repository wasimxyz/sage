title: Flat tire
---

Fixed a flat on the bike myself, with a video open on the phone propped against the wall. It took ninety minutes. The first fifteen were me trying to get the wheel off. The tube was fine, it was the valve, so I replaced the whole tube, pumped it up, and then pumped it up again because I didn't trust it. It's held for six hours so far. I now own a tire lever and I'm weirdly attached to it.
