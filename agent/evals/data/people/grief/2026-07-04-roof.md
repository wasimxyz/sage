date: 2026-07-04
title: Roof
---

Mr. Okafor's daughter got the roof door unlocked and about eight of us from the building watched the fireworks from up there. Somebody brought a cooler and somebody else brought a watermelon and cut it on the ledge, which felt unwise, but the watermelon went fine. You could see two displays at once, one over the river and one past the bridge, and they were slightly out of time with each other.

Came down at eleven with sticky hands and a sunburn on the back of my neck. Mr. Okafor said we should do this every year. I said I'd bring the napkins.
