Cleared out the hall closet. Three winter coats I don't wear, a box of cables for devices I no longer own, two umbrellas that don't open, and a bowling ball I don't remember buying. The coats went in a bag for the donation bin at the end of the block. The ball is still in the hallway because I can't think what to do with it. Marcus says it's worth something. I don't believe him.
