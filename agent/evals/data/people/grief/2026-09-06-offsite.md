title: Offsite
---

Team offsite on Friday at a place two hours north with a ropes course. I was told it would be "light and fun." What it was, was a wooden wall that your whole group had to get over without using the sides. Marcus ended up on top because he is tall, and a woman from legal told everyone where to put their feet, and it worked, mostly. I got to the top second to last and sat there for a second looking at the trees before somebody pulled me over.

Lunch was a sandwich on a paper plate under a tent. A guy from the other floor asked what I do and I said "spreadsheets" and he said "me too," and that was the whole conversation, and it was fine. The facilitator made us go around the circle and say one word for how we felt. Eleven people said "energized." I said "fed." Then we each wrote one goal for the quarter on a sticky note and put it on a wall. Mine said "finish things." Someone in the next group wrote "synergy" and a few people nodded at it like it was a real goal.

The bus ride back was the best part. Everyone fell asleep except the driver and me. I counted six people with their mouths open. Marcus was one of them and he'll deny it.
