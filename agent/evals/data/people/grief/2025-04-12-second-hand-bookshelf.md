title: Bookshelf
---

Bought a bookshelf off a guy online and Dev helped me carry it up three flights. By helped I mean he held the door and said "your left, your left" a lot. It's the tall kind with five shelves. The bottom one wobbles, so I folded a piece of cardboard under one corner, which I would have been embarrassed by a few years ago and now just feel pleased with.

Cookbooks went low since I actually use them. The paperbacks I haven't opened in ten years went up top, where I can't reach them and don't have to have feelings about them.
