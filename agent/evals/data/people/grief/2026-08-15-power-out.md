date: 2026-08-15
title: Power out
---

A storm took the power out around six and it stayed out until almost midnight. I lit the two candles I own and read on the floor by the window until it got too dark to see the page. Then I sat there listening to the rain and to everyone on the street shouting at each other about whether their lights were out too. Nobody could tell. The fridge was fine when the power came back, thank God, because I'd just bought groceries. I didn't open it once the whole time.
