date: 2025-08-02
title: Claire in town
---

Claire came up for the weekend and we took the ferry out to the island, the one with the clam shack and the lighthouse nobody is allowed inside. She talked the whole way over about her landlord, who has decided the lawn is her responsibility. I talked about the new desks and Marcus and the snack schedule. It was nice to complain about small things to someone who has to listen.

Got a sunburn on exactly one shoulder. Claire says it's because I always stand on the same side of the boat. She's right, I do. We ate fried clams on the dock and a seagull took a roll right off her plate, and she yelled at it for a lot longer than the roll was worth. She's still mad about it. She brought it up again on the ferry back.

At my place she made us eggs at nine, because we'd eaten clams at three and neither of us wanted a real meal. She slept on the couch, took the blue blanket, and wouldn't give it back in the morning, which she has been doing since she was nine. I walked her to the train at noon. She waved from the window until the platform ran out.
