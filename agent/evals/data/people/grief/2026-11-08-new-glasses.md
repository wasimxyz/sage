title: New glasses
---

Picked up the new glasses. I told the optician I wanted the same frames as the old ones, and she said "are you sure," and I said yes, and she looked a little sad about it. The difference is that I can see the leaves on the trees across the street now. I'd forgotten they had individual leaves.

Wore them to the grocery store and home again, and kept taking them off to look at the old pair.
