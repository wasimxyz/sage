title: Lentil soup
---

Made lentil soup from a recipe I found online. It said two teaspoons of cumin. I used two tablespoons because I misread it, and I didn't notice until I tasted it. Added a diced potato and a splash of red wine vinegar and it came back to something you could eat.

For the record: one onion, two carrots, three cloves of garlic, a cup of brown lentils, six cups of water, a bay leaf, salt. The cumin is a suggestion. It makes about six lunches, which I need, because I told myself I'd stop buying a sandwich every day. I've made that promise before. This time there's a pot of soup in the fridge, so it's different.
