Finally replaced the phone with the cracked screen. The old one had been holding on for two years and the battery was down to about an hour by lunchtime. The guy at the store moved everything over while I stood there, and then I spent the afternoon at home finding out what hadn't come with it. The codes for my accounts, the transit app, every alarm I ever set.

Had to rebuild my alarms from memory. I still don't know what the 6:15 one was for, so I didn't bring it back.
