title: Bad dream
---

Dreamed the hospital called about Claire. Not a long dream. The phone was ringing in the kitchen of the old house, the one with the yellow curtains, and I couldn't make my hand pick it up, and a voice said "is this the family of" and I woke up. 4:50 by the clock. Lay there with my heart going.

I held out until 6:40 and then texted her. She wrote back "it's Monday. why are you awake." Then a photo of her coffee on the porch. I said bad dream, nothing important. She said "tell me at lunch," and I did, a little, and she said she's fine, she's boring, she has a 9:30 about a budget.

Tired all day. Marcus asked if I'd slept and I said I'd had a dream. He said he once dreamed he showed up to work as a lobster. We agreed that was worse.
