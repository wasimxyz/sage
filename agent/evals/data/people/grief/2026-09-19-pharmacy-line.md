date: 2026-09-19
title: Pharmacy line
---

Picked up allergy pills this morning. Line of five. The woman in front of me was older, with a canvas bag of soup cans, and she was telling the girl at the register about her husband's refills. "He passed in March," she said, "and they still send me the notices." The girl said she could take him off the list. The woman said no, leave him on, it's the only mail that comes addressed to both of us. She paid in coins. She counted the total out loud, twice.

Then it was my turn. I didn't say anything to her. It didn't seem like mine to say anything about.

Pills were nine dollars. Went to the market and bought pears and a loaf that was still warm and ate the heel in the car.
