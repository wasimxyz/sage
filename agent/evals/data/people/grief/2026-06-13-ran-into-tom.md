title: Ran into Tom
---

Ran into Tom from my old team at the grocery store, by the olive oil. We haven't talked since he left two years ago. He's at a startup now, has a baby, looks like he sleeps four hours a night, and is happy about all of it. We stood in the aisle for fifteen minutes. He said we should get lunch and I said yes, and we both know that isn't happening, but it was nice of us.

Bought the wrong olive oil because I was too polite to check the label while he was still standing there.
