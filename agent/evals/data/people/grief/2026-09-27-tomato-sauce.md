title: Tomatoes
---

Went to the market and a woman was selling a box of tomatoes too soft for her to sell properly, so she gave me the whole box for almost nothing. Spent the afternoon making sauce. Boiled them, slipped the skins off, which is the satisfying part, and then spent two hours stirring with the windows open because it was 85 outside and the kitchen was a sauna. Ended up with seven jars, kind of. Four are full and the rest are "half jars," which I am going to claim was on purpose.

Gave one to Mr. Okafor. He said it needed salt. It does.
