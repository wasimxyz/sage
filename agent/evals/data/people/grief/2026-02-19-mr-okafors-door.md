date: 2026-02-19
title: Mr. Okafor's door
---

His newspaper was on the mat Tuesday, Wednesday, and again this morning, still in the plastic sleeve. By the time I got to the stairs I had a whole list going. He's seventy-nine. His hip is bad. If he fell in the bathroom on Sunday, nobody would know for days, and I didn't want to be the person who thought that and then walked past.

Knocked. Knocked again. Heard the TV through the door. Then he opened up in his cardigan, a little annoyed, and said he'd been at his daughter's since Sunday and only got back at midnight. He'd forgotten to stop the paper. I said that was all I needed to know. He asked if I wanted tea and I said no, and then yes, and I sat at his table for twenty minutes while he told me about his daughter's cat, who has opinions about the stairs.

Went back up with the list still in my head, smaller. Left a note under his door with my number, in case. He wrote on the same note, "Tea next Thursday?" and slid it back under mine.
