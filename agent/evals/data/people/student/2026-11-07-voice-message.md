Ibu sent a voice message, four minutes. She doesn't like to type. She talked about the rain starting at home, the neighbor's wedding, Rizky who is now taller than Bapak's shoulder. Then she asked, "Kakak, are you eating well? Do you wear the jacket? Do you have friends there?" I listened three times, once with headphones in bed with the light off, so her voice was right next to my ear.

I wrote back: "Yes Ibu, eating well, jacket is warm, have many friends." Two true things and one not exactly. The one not exactly is friends. I have Marta, and Tomas at work, and Hana but far. It is not nothing. But "many" is a big word. I don't want Ibu to worry. She cannot do anything from there except worry.
