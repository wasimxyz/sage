title: Two coffees
---

A woman came to the café at three, when it is slow. She ordered one flat white and one black coffee, two cups. I thought somebody is coming after her. Then she said, "My husband died in March. He took it black. I still order for him, it is a habit." She said it calm, like she was telling the time.

I said I am sorry. It is the only thing I could say, and it came out in a very small voice. I gave her the black one first. She smiled a little and sat by the window with both cups for one hour. She drank only the flat white. When I wiped the next table I saw the black one still full, and next to it one dollar for tip.

I did not throw it away until the end of the shift. I don't know why. Dina saw and did not say anything, which I was thankful for. Kangen home a bit today, a strange kangen, for nothing in particular.
