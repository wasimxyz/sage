title: The ticket
---

I checked the ticket home again. $1,380 for the end of June, coming back in August. I looked at the number for a long time, like it can change if I wait.

Then I called Ibu. It is already tomorrow there, she was at the market and I could hear the motorbikes behind her. I said the ticket is not so expensive, which is a small lie. She said no, no, don't come. Save the money, she said, the scholarship does not pay for the flight, and Bapak thinks it is better to work in the summer and learn from it. She said all this very fast, like she had it ready before I called.

I said okay Ibu, you are right. Then we talked about Rizky's exam, and the neighbor's cat who had four kittens. After the call I sat on the bed with the ticket page still open.

I will not buy it. Ibu is right. I only did not know how much I wanted her to say, come, I will cook.
