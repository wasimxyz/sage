title: Call with Bapak
---

Last night I had a video call with Bapak, so it was already Sunday morning for him and he wore his good shirt. Ibu was behind him with a pot, waving a spoon at the camera. He asked the first thing right away: what is your rank in the class? I told him they do not give rank here, only grades. Then who is the top, he asked. I said I don't know, maybe Marta.

Then he told me Anton, my cousin, was number one in his whole faculty and the family is talking about it. He did not say it to be mean. He says things like this the way people say the weather.

He asked about the B+ in calculus. I said it was a hard class and the professor is strict. He said a hard class is a reason and not an excuse. I said yes, Bapak. I said "yes" maybe five times in the call. After, I looked at my face on the screen and I was smiling, like the smile was stuck there.
