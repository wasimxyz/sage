title: No
---

Tomas asked me to cover his Wednesday shift. I said, "I can't this time, I have a statistics exam on Thursday." I said it out loud before I could change it. He said "oh no worries, all good" in about one second. Maybe less. That is all that happened.

I felt bad for two hours anyway. I wrote the sentence in my head five different ways to see if there is a nicer way to say no. I even checked my phone to see if he sent something angry. He sent a sticker of a dancing sandwich.

At the library I told Marta. She said it's normal, a lot of people feel guilty for no. I asked is it normal to feel guilty for a long time. She said I can try again and see. Maybe I will.
