title: Missed the bus
---

Missed the last bus after the late shift, so I walked 40 minutes. A light rain, not enough for an umbrella but enough to be annoying. The bakery near the dorm was closing and the man gave me one roll for free, I don't know why. Maybe I looked sad. At the dorm I put my socks on the heater and the shoes upside down on newspaper. Jae was out. The room was quiet and I liked it. I ate the roll in bed with the light off.
