title: First shift
---

First day at the café in the student union. Dina trained me, and Tomas also, who is slow and funny. The milk steamer is so loud, I jumped the first time and the milk jug almost left my hand. There are sixteen kinds of drinks and I wrote them on my hand to remember. I made three mistakes: two lattes with the wrong milk and one lid upside down. Dina said everyone does that in the first week. I said sorry maybe eleven times. She said, you don't have to say sorry every time. I said sorry.

Six hours standing, my feet are done. But the pay is better than the library and at the end of the shift we get one free drink. I drank mine slowly on the bench outside. It was the first coffee in many days that somebody else made for me.
