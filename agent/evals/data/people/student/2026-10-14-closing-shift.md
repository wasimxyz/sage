Closing shift with Tomas. We counted the till twice because it was 35 cents off, and then found the coins under the tip jar. Dina wrote on the whiteboard "the 35 cent mystery, solved." We wiped every table and mopped. Tomas told a long story about his cousin's wedding where the cake fell and the best man ate the part that fell. I laughed more than the story needed. I think because I did not laugh for some days.

Tomas asked if I can open on Friday. I said, let me check my schedule. This is new. I checked my schedule and said yes anyway, but "let me check" felt a bit different in my mouth.
