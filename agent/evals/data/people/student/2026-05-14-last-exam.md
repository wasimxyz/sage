title: Last exam
---

Last exam today, statistics. I thought I would feel happy when it finished. I feel nothing. I walked out and stood in front of the building for some time, and everybody around me was taking pictures and jumping. I clapped for Hana when she came out, because she looked like she wanted somebody to clap.

Hana invited all the floor to the noodle place near the dorm. I was very tired and wanted to sleep but I said yes, of course. The table was loud. Everybody was talking about their flight home, Saturday, Sunday, next Wednesday. When it was my turn I said "maybe later, still checking" and nobody asked more.

Back in the room at 11. The hallway already looks different, doors open and boxes everywhere.
