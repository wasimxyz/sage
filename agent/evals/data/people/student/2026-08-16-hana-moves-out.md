title: Hana's boxes
---

Helped Hana move to her apartment. It's 20 minutes on the bus, she shares with a girl from her chemistry class, and the balcony is the size of a table. I carried boxes up three floors, and then also the roommate's boxes, because she asked and it was easy to say yes. Hana was happy and sweaty. She said, come every weekend! I said yes, definitely. I think we both know it will be maybe once a month.

Took the bus back alone. Almost empty, only a man asleep with a guitar case. I looked out of the window and thought, now who will I eat noodles with. A small thought, but it stayed with me the whole evening.

The dorm hallway smells like new paint. They are painting before the students come back.
