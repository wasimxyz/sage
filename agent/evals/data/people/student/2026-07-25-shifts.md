title: Shifts
---

Tomas asked me to take his Saturday shift because his cousin is visiting. I said yes. Then Dina asked if I can stay Monday evening because someone is sick. I said yes. Then Tomas asked for Thursday also. I said yes. I counted: six days this week. Today my feet are like two stones.

I don't know why I say yes so fast. In my head I say "no, I have a lot to do" but my mouth says "sure, no problem". I think it is sungkan. The other person already asks and I cannot make the face of no.

Tomas bought me a muffin. It was a good muffin. It was not worth Thursday.
