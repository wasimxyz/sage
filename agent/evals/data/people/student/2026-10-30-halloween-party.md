Jae had a Halloween party in our room. I planned to be in the library but Jae said, you live here too! so I stayed. Costumes: a taco, two people in a horse together, a girl dressed as a smoke alarm. I wore a bedsheet. Marta came as a real ghost with chains. I stood next to the snacks and held a cup of juice all night so nobody asks me to dance.

I talked to three people for a few minutes each. One asked where I am from and said, oh that's so cool, do you have beaches? I said yes, many. He said I'd love to visit, and I said you should! I always say "you should" and I don't know if I mean it.

At 11:30 I went to sit in the study lounge. It was quiet and nice. When I came back the party was still going.
