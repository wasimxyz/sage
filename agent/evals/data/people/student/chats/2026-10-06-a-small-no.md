title: A sentence for saying no
---
User: Hi. I want to practice saying no. Tomas always asks me to cover his shifts. I always say yes. I don't want.
Sage: That sounds tiring. What happens in your head when he asks?
User: I think no. My mouth says sure. Sungkan. It is like I cannot make the no face.
Sage: Maybe the first no can be very small and kind. Would one short sentence help, like "That doesn't work for me, but thank you for asking"? You don't need to give a reason.
User: "That doesn't work for me, but thank you for asking." I like this. It is not rude.
Sage: Right. It says no without a fight, and it is true.
User: Even if my heart is loud, at least the words are ready.
Sage: Exactly. Having the words ready counts as a win.
User: Okay. I write on my hand: doesn't work for me.
Sage: That's a good reminder. How do you think it will feel to say it?
