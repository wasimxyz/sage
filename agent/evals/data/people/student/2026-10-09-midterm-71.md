Midterm is back. 71 out of 100. Professor Reyes wrote the class average on the board, 68, and said anything over 60 is solid. Marta said congratulations, you are above average! I said thank you and smiled. After class I stood in a stall in the bathroom for some minutes with my face hot.

I know 71 is not failing. I know it in my head. My stomach doesn't know, it thinks I failed something big. Above average is not what I came here for. The scholarship, the whole year I worked for this.

Went to the café and made 40 drinks. At the end Dina said I was quiet today. I said I was concentrating.
