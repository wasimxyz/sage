SUNDOWNING: WHAT HELPS
Thursday Caregivers' Group, Hospital Annex

Late afternoon and evening can bring more confusion, restlessness, and worry for people living with dementia. You are not causing it, and you may not be able to stop it. These steps may help.

- Keep the late afternoon calm. Turn on the lights before it gets dark and close the curtains at dusk.
- Do the hard things (bathing, appointments) in the morning.
- Offer a snack and something to drink around three. Skip caffeine after noon.
- Play familiar music or a favorite show at a low volume.
- Don't argue. If your loved one says they need to go home, ask them to tell you about home.
- Hand the evening to someone else one night a week if you can.
