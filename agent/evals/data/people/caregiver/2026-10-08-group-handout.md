title: Group, the handout
---

Group night. Pat handed out a sheet called "Warning Signs of Caregiver Stress," twelve bullets. I met nine. I read down the list and thought, so this is how you find out you're a case study. The ones I'll admit to: isolation, irritability, trouble sleeping, health problems you're ignoring, and resentment toward the person you care for. I ticked that last one and then made a little hatch mark beside it, in case it needed to be counted twice.

We did a "self-care plate," a drawing of a plate divided into slices. Mine had one slice, labeled "sleep." Bernadette put "bath." Walt put "Joanne." Nobody laughed. I wanted to leave the room.

I rolled my eyes at the plate. Quietly, but Pat saw. She smiled at me like I was the ringleader of a small rebellion. I think I may like her, which isn't helpful.
