title: Door chime
---

I found her in the front yard at ten past five this morning. Nightgown, one slipper, standing at the edge of the lawn looking down the street. She said she was waiting for the school bus and had she missed it. I said not yet, let's go in and have toast. She came, easily, and I got her inside and then my knees went. I sat on the kitchen floor.

She is fine. She's asleep now, and I've checked that she's asleep roughly eleven times. The door had been locked. She'd turned the bolt, which is a thing her hands still remember how to do. I called in to the clinic and spent the morning at the hardware store buying chimes for both doors and a gadget for the gate, and I installed them with a drill while Yolanda kept Mom company. The first chime went off when Yolanda took out the trash, and I almost threw up.

Safe enough now, I told myself. I don't think I'll sleep through anything again.
