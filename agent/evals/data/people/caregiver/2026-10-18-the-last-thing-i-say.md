title: The last thing I say
---

Snapped at her this afternoon. She asked what day it was for what I think was the fortieth time, and I said "I already told you," in a voice I don't use on dogs. She flinched. A small flinch, like I'd dropped something. Then she said, "I'm sorry, dear," and that was so much worse.

I apologized right away. She'd already forgotten, which is a mercy and a robbery. But I couldn't let it go, and tonight I lay in bed doing the thing my old therapist would have circled in pencil. What if the last thing I ever say to her is "I already told you." What if she dies in the night and that's the voice she takes with her. I listened for her breathing through the wall like an idiot. Catastrophizing, fortune telling, all-or-nothing, I can name every distortion. Naming them is not the same as being able to turn them off.

She is fine. She's asleep in the next room. At 11:40 I got up and stood in her doorway until I saw the blanket move, and then I went back to bed and did it again at two. That's a nervous system, not a prophecy. Nothing has happened. I keep telling myself that, because the only evidence I have is the blanket.
