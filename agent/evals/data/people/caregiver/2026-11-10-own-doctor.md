title: Own doctor
---

Called my own doctor's office. First available is January 19th. I took it. The receptionist asked if it was urgent and I said no, and then she asked what it was for and I said "a checkup," and I could hear how that sounded, a forty-seven-year-old booking two months out for a checkup. Blood pressure, probably. I fell asleep sitting up in a waiting room last year, so.

I put it in the calendar in a color I don't use. Then I texted Neil that I'd booked it, as if that were news for him. He sent back a thumbs-up. I looked at the thumbs-up for a while.
