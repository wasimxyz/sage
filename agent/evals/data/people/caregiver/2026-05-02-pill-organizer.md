title: Pill organizer
---

Filled the pill organizer, the big one with four slots a day. Twenty-eight compartments. I've done it every Saturday for fourteen months and I still check it twice, because the one time I didn't, she got Tuesday night's pills on Wednesday morning and was out of it until noon. Nothing came of it, the pharmacist said. I know nothing came of it. The checking isn't about the pills. My old therapist would say I'm managing anxiety through procedure, and she'd be right, and the pills would still need checking.

Mom watched me from the table and asked if I was a nurse. I said sort of. She said, "You're good at it." That's what gets me, those little flashes where she's right there. I put the Thursday lid on wrong. Twice.
