date: 2026-08-11
title: After Neil
---

Neil left yesterday morning. Mom spent most of the afternoon looking for him. "Where's the nice man?" Not "where's my son." Just the nice man, who brought bagels. I told her he went home. She said, "Oh, that's a shame, he was lovely."

I'm trying to say this without it sounding petty: I'd have liked it if the nice man had asked, one time in four days, what my week looks like. He asked what my plan is for "when it gets worse." He asked it in the doorway with his bag on his shoulder. I said I didn't have one. He said, "We should figure that out," and then the cab honked.

That "we" is doing a lot of work.
