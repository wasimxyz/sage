title: Shower
---

Showers are Tuesday and Saturday. Mom let me wash her hair today and hummed something from the fifties, and for a few minutes we were parent and child in the oldest arrangement, her head tipped back, me with the cup. I could have stayed there. Then halfway through she said, very politely, "I'm sorry, who are you?" and pulled the towel up. I said I was her helper and could we finish the rinse. She said yes, dear.

I finished with the water going cold. I dried her hair. I put on her lavender lotion, which she likes, which she says belongs to somebody else. I cried in the hall closet afterward for exactly as long as it takes a kettle to boil, because I could hear it from there.

There's a word for it when a child ends up parenting a parent, and I know I've got the arrow backwards here, because she's still the one who did it first. I don't think there's a word for being a stranger to the person who first learned your face. If there is, I don't want to look it up.
