title: Front desk
---

A woman brought in her fifteen-year-old cat today to be put to sleep, and left with an empty carrier. I was good. Calm voice, tissues, the paperwork, offered to walk her out. She told me the cat had been with her through a divorce and a move and she didn't know how she'd get home without it in the carrier. I said we could sit a minute. I was competent and kind. Dolores gave me a look that was almost gratitude.

I drove home and sat in the driveway and felt nothing at all. Not sad, not numb exactly, more like the part of me that cries has been moved to the back of a long line behind more urgent things. Anticipatory grief is the term. I know the term. I read the book. It didn't mention the part where you're better at other people's losses than your own because you've had so much practice staying upright.

Mom's inside right now watching her show. She's fine, and I'm sitting outside in a car.
