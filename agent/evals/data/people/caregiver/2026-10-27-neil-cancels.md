date: 2026-10-27
title: Neil cancels
---

Neil texted at 2:15 this afternoon. "Hey, so bad news, work blew up, can we push the week with Mom to January? So sorry!! You're a rock." He was supposed to take her for the first week of November. I'd already lined up a dentist appointment, a haircut, and a long, stupid nap.

I wrote back "Okay." Then I wrote a long reply and deleted it, and then a shorter one and deleted that, and put the phone in a drawer. I made dinner. I cut the crusts off Mom's sandwich the way she likes and my hand was shaking.

"You're a rock" is what people say to a rock. I know how this works: the person who's always there gets treated like furniture. I can recite the family-systems chapter. I also know I never told him the week mattered. I let it be a favor. I don't have a script for being angry at someone and also owning my part in it. I'm too tired to be generous. I'll be generous anyway, probably, because the alternative is another dead end.
