title: Yolanda
---

Yolanda has been coming Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays since February, and what I can't get past is how much I look forward to her car pulling into the driveway. Not because I like her, although I do. Because for five hours somebody else is the one who answers the question about what day it is. Yolanda has a way of saying "well, let's see" that makes Mom feel like it's a game.

I feel disloyal about it. I know the sentence I'd say to a friend: it's okay to want relief, relief isn't betrayal. I can say it perfectly. It lands nowhere.

At work today I sat in my car in the clinic lot for ten minutes before going in, just because nobody could reach me there. Dolores asked if I'd been waiting for someone. I said no, just finishing a podcast. I wasn't listening to anything.
