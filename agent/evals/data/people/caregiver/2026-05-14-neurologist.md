title: Neurologist
---

Appointment with the neurologist at ten. He said moderate stage, in the tone of someone reading a menu. Adjusted one medication and added a second, with a list of side effects I'll be watching for at 3 a.m. like it's a job. I asked what to expect over the next six months and he said "it varies," which is true and no use at all.

Mom charmed the waiting room. She told the receptionist her hair was lovely and asked the man across from us if he was somebody's husband. He said he was. She said, "Lucky woman." Everyone laughed, including me.

In the car she said, "Thank you for taking me out, sweetheart," and then she said my name. I'll hang on to that. I picked up a pamphlet called "Planning for the Next Stage" and put it in the glove box without reading it. I know that's avoidance. I'm naming it so it counts.
