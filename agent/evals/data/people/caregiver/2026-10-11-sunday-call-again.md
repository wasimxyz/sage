title: Sunday call again
---

Neil called at four, for nine minutes. I could set a clock by it. This time he'd been "thinking things through." He asked, carefully, whether I'd "looked into memory care at all, just so we know what the options are." I said I had a pamphlet. He said, "Okay, good, because honestly, you're carrying so much." He offered to pay a third. A third. I said I'd look at numbers and send them. He said, "Great, just send me whatever you find."

I didn't say anything about the other two thirds, or the nights, or that he'd said "we should figure this out" in August and it's October. I said, "Mom says hi." Mom was in the other room humming along to the radio and hadn't said anything of the kind.

I'm leaving the Birchwood tour on the calendar for Thursday. Neil gets to find out what it costs the same way I did.
