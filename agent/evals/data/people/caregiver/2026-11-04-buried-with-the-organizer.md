date: 2026-11-04
title: Buried with the organizer
---

Told Yolanda this morning that at this rate I'll die first and they'll have to bury me with the pill organizer in my hands. She laughed so hard she had to sit on the arm of the couch. Then she stopped and asked when I last saw my own doctor, which was fair, and which I'd rather not answer.

Two years, give or take. Mom's records I can recite. Mine I couldn't tell you.

Mom, from the kitchen: "What's funny?" Yolanda said, "This one's a comedian." Mom said, "Always has been," and I wasn't sure who she meant. Then the pharmacy called about a refill and I wrote it on my hand.
