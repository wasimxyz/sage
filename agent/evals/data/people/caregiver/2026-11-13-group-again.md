Went last night. Walt wasn't there. Pat said he was "taking a night," in the voice that means don't ask. I felt a pang about it, and I'm surprised how much. Bernadette had a new bag of the same cookies and gave me two for the road.

At the end she wrote her number on a napkin and said, "In case you ever want to say something to someone who doesn't need you to be okay." I put it in my coat pocket. I didn't throw it away at the curb, which was my plan. It's still there, and I haven't decided what it is yet.

Yolanda was in the chair when I got home, with the TV on low and Mom asleep. I thanked her too much. She said, "Go to bed."
