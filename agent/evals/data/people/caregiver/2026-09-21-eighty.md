date: 2026-09-21
title: Eighty
---

Mom turned eighty. I took the day off and got a small cake from the bakery on Dunmore, the lemon one, and Yolanda stayed through the afternoon so it would feel like a party. Neil called and sang on speaker, off-key. Mom listened with her head tilted. At the end she said, "Thank you, that was lovely," to the phone, and then, "Who was that?"

She blew out the candles with me. She said, "Happy birthday, sweetheart," and I realized she thought it was mine. I said thank you. I didn't correct it. We ate the cake with our hands.

Later Yolanda said, "You look like you need a hug, but I'm going to pretend I didn't see that." I said the same applied to her. Eighty is a lot of years. She still has most of her laugh.
