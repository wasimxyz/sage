date: 2026-06-27
title: Where's Frank
---

She asked for Dad four times today. "Where's Frank? He's late." Dad died in 2019. The pamphlet says don't correct her, step into her world, redirect. I said he's at work and he'll be home soon and she settled and asked what's for dinner. It worked. It feels like lying anyway.

The honest option is telling her he's gone, and she cries like it's the first time because to her it is. I did that once, in the winter, and watched her find out. I won't do it again. So I lie, and the guidelines say the lie is kind, and at the sink afterward I realized I'd been saying "he'll be home soon" for so long that some part of me looked at the door.

I'm not sure that's grief. It might just be a reflex. Either way I stood there with a wet plate in my hand for a while.
