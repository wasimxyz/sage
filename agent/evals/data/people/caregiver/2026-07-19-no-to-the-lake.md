title: Said no to the lake
---

Mira invited me to her family's lake house for a weekend in August. Three nights, a canoe, nobody asking me what day it is. I said I'd have to see about coverage. Yolanda said she could do a weekend if I paid for it, and I did the math, and the math isn't impossible. It's that I'd be gone and something would happen and I'd have been the person who went.

Mira texted, "whenever you're ready, no pressure!!" The "whenever" is the part I can't stand. There isn't a version of this where I'm more ready later. The pressure is already here. It lives in my kitchen.

I told her next summer. She sent a heart. I know I'm doing the thing where I say no now to avoid the guilt I'd feel if I said yes. Knowing the mechanism hasn't loosened it.
