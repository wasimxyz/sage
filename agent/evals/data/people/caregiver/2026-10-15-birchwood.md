title: Birchwood
---

Toured Birchwood this morning. It's the one with the good reviews. Pretty lobby, a fish tank, a woman in a blazer who said "we like to say it's a community" within thirty seconds. The memory wing has a door you press a code into. The residents were at a sing-along around a piano. One man was conducting with a spoon. The place smelled like soup and floor cleaner. It was fine. It was honestly fine, and I hated it, and I'm trying not to read that as evidence of anything.

$7,800 a month. The blazer said it the way you'd mention the weather. Mom's pension and savings would cover about fourteen months of that. Even split with Neil it's tight, and I know how much I can count on from Neil.

I asked to see a room. A bed, a window, a framed print of a barn. I stood there picturing Mom's afghan on the bed and found I couldn't picture her in it. I could only picture me visiting, which is worse, I think. I put the brochure in the glove box with the other one. The glove box is becoming a very specific archive.
