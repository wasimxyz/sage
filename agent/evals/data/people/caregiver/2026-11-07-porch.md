title: Porch
---

Raked leaves. Mom sat on the porch in two blankets and her good hat and supervised. At some point she started the story about Dad's truck and the flat tire outside Dayton, the one I've heard maybe two hundred times. I know where she pauses. I know which part she gets wrong now (the color of the truck, it was blue, she says green). I said "What did you do?" at the right moment, and she did the little shrug she does, and finished it.

It was a good hour. I don't want to make it mean something. I don't want to write that it was a gift, or that I'll cherish it, because that's the version I'd tell at group, and Pat would nod, and it would turn into a sentence. It was an hour. The leaves were wet. She was cold and said so, and I brought another blanket.
