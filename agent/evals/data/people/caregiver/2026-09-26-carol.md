title: Carol
---

I said I wouldn't write about yesterday, and then I woke up at five with the sentences already formed. So.

She didn't know me. All day, from breakfast on. She called me Carol, which is her sister, who died in 2021. Then she asked me, very politely, to call her a cab because she didn't know me and wanted to go home. I said we'd go after dinner. I kept saying after dinner. At six she took her pills from my hand like a stranger giving me the benefit of the doubt.

What I mean by lost is not that she died. She's here. She's in the next room right now. What I mean is that I'm in my own house taking care of a woman who has been very good to me and has no idea. I can't say this to anyone without sounding dramatic. People say, "At least she's still with you." Yes. At least. It's what I say too.

She knew me for a minute at lunch today. She said, "You're tired, aren't you?" I said yes. That was it, and she went back to her toast.
