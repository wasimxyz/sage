title: Saturday at the store
---

Saturday at the store. A man came in at 8:05 wanting "a thing for a thing" and drew a shape in the air. I sold him a P-trap. He left happy, so maybe it was the thing. Cut 41 keys. Mixed seven gallons of a green called Fern Hollow, which is the color of every hospital hallway I've ever been in. Big Mike spent his lunch break arguing with the vending machine. He's about five-foot-four, so the name is a joke, and he'd like it if people stopped pointing that out.

Closed at six. Eggs over the sink. Normal. I love normal. I'm bored by normal. Both of those, same hour.
