title: 3 a.m.
---

3:10 a.m. and I'm wide awake with my brain playing the highlight reel, the worst moments, narrated, with commentary. The time I called Mom at midnight and cried about a pizza. The wedding I left early. The night at the Kowalskis'. I've apologized for all of it, officially, in writing. The reel doesn't care. It's a streaming service with no cancel button.

I got up and ate toast standing in the dark kitchen. Texted nobody. Read the back of a cereal box about the founder's vision. Tried the breathing thing Ruth taught me and fell asleep around five on the couch with the box on my chest.

Woke at 7 and it was a perfectly nice Sunday. The reel had gone off the air. Some mornings the whole thing feels like a rumor.
