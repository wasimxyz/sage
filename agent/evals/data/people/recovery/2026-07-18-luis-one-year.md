date: 2026-07-18
title: Luis, one year
---

Luis got his one-year chip on Thursday. Ruth handed it to him. He cried a little and blamed the donuts. Seventeen of us in the basement in a circle, holding hands for the prayer, like the world's most sober wedding.

He asked me to say a few words. I said he walked in last July on day two, white as paper, and sat next to me and said nothing for an hour. I was four months in, and felt like a veteran, which is hilarious now. I said it was the best thing anyone had done for me that month. He said "you're making me cry again" and threw a donut at me.

In the parking lot afterward I felt hopeful so hard it was almost embarrassing. Like everything I've been doing is pointing somewhere. One year. A whole year. Then I got in the car and it faded to normal, which is fine. Normal is fine.
