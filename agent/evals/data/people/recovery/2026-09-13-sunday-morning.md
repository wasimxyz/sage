title: Sunday morning
---

I drank last night.

Dinner was fine for the first hour. Dan ordered a bottle of the red he always used to pick, and I said no thanks and asked for a lemon in my water, and he said "good for you" and I think he meant it. He told a story about his brother's divorce that was so funny I laughed until the table shook. It was the first time I'd laughed like that in a restaurant in a year and a half. Then he went outside to take a call, and the glass he'd poured for me out of habit was sitting there. I drank it. I don't have a better way to say it. I picked it up and drank it. By the time he was back I'd poured another.

Two glasses each, more or less. He left at ten for his hotel. I stood outside the restaurant, walked to the corner store, bought the cheapest red they had, and drank most of it at home on the couch with my shoes on. I woke at five with the lamp still on and the bottle on the floor and the taste in my mouth.

It was a very mediocre merlot. That's the part I keep coming back to. It tasted like warm cough syrup.

I haven't told anyone. Not Ruth, not Luis. Writing it here first. It's day one, I think. I'll figure out the count when I can look at the number without my eyes sliding off it.
