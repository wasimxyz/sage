title: Dan at the store
---

Dan walked into the store at 10:15 for a cabinet hinge. I watched him come down aisle six from the paint counter. Same walk, same jacket, same way of looking at shelves as if they owe him something. I could have gone into the stockroom. I stood there like a mannequin instead until he saw me.

It was fine. It was polite, even. He asked how I was, I said good, he said I looked good, I said thanks. His kitchen is falling apart. I walked him to the hinges. He was holding a travel mug, and I did what I always do now, which is look at the mug and then at him, and I couldn't tell. It isn't mine to track anymore. He said "I'm glad you're doing the thing" and I said "me too." He paid and left. He didn't mention the texts, and neither did I.

Then I spent the afternoon in a state I can only call a hangover without the drinking. Big Mike asked if I'd seen a ghost. I said an ex. He said that's worse.
