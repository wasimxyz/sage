title: Coffee with Ruth
---

Coffee with Ruth at the diner. She orders the same grilled cheese every time and cuts it into exactly four triangles. Twenty-two years sober and still exacting. We went over the step work, the part where you write down who you've hurt. My list is longer than I want to say out loud. She told me I don't have to do the whole thing this week, that I can do one line. One line I can do. I'm great at one line.

She also reminded me about HALT, which is hungry, angry, lonely, tired. I asked what if I'm all four, as a bundle. She laughed and said then eat first. So I had pie. It's hard to feel hopeless with pie in front of you. Not impossible. Hard.
