Two months today. Day 62 if you're counting, and I'm counting. Luis wants to celebrate with a cake. Ruth says two months isn't a milestone, it's a Friday. Both of them are right, which is why I love and resent them.

Thanksgiving is in two weeks at Jess's, and I've been planning it like a heist. I'll bring the sparkling cider, take the chair near the door, leave by four. Jess already said she's not putting out wine. I told her she doesn't need to do that. She said "I know. I want to." I said okay and went out to the car and sat there a while.

Some days this feels like the best I've ever done at anything. Some days it feels like walking a very long fence. Today is a fence day with a nice view.
