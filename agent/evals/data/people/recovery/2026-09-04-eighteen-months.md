title: Eighteen months
---

Eighteen months today. Ruth gave me the chip at the meeting last night, a day early because there's no meeting on Fridays. Bronze, with the triangle. I flipped it in my pocket the whole walk to the car and then lost it inside my own jacket lining for ten minutes. I am a person who can lose a coin that is in my pocket.

In the circle I said I don't think about drinking anymore. That's not totally true. It's more that I think about it and it doesn't win. Ruth looked at me a second too long and said "good" in a way that made me think she'd heard the first sentence and not the second.

Called Mom to tell her. She cried and then asked if I'd eaten. Everyone in my life has the same one question.
