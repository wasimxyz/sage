title: Heat
---

96 degrees in the store, and the air conditioning has been "being looked at" since Tuesday. We sold forty-two fans and one pool noodle. By noon the fans were gone so I started telling people they were "in transit," which is a lie, and a boring one. A woman begged for one for her dog and I gave her the display model, which had a sticker on it that said NOT FOR SALE. Big Mike says that's either theft or kindness, and nobody can tell him which.

At home I ate a popsicle on the bathroom floor, the coolest room, and called it dinner. Second popsicle was dessert. Watched a bird in the neighbor's tree sit there panting with its beak open. We were having the same night.
