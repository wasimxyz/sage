title: Dan
---

Dan texted at 11:40 last night. First time since February. A photo of the bar where we had our first date, the neon sign half burnt out, and under it, "miss you, you jerk." I read it in the bathroom at work. Ten minutes later: "come out next friday. one drink isn't going to kill you."

I put the phone in my locker and made a very aggressive sign for the paint aisle (PLEASE DO NOT OPEN THE CANS). I haven't answered. Part of me is proud and part of me has read the texts four times, so it's a split decision.

Nobody tells you that when you leave someone who still drinks, they're still funny. He was always funny. That was the problem with the whole thing.

In bed I thought I'm so strong, and then I thought I'm one text away from being a person at that bar, and both of those were true at 1 a.m. By morning the second one had got smaller. Not gone.
