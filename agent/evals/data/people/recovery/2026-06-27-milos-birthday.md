date: 2026-06-27
title: Milo is five
---

Milo's fifth birthday at Jess's. Backyard, a bounce house, twenty kids and maybe eleven adults holding beers like they'd been issued them. I counted the coolers. Three. I brought a case of lime seltzer and put it on the picnic table like a flag. Jess's husband handed me a bottle without looking and I handed it back without looking and neither of us said a word, which is how it works now.

Stayed forty minutes longer than I wanted to. Helped with the cake, shaped like a dog, looked like a bear. Milo blew out the candles and said his wish out loud, which ruins it, and the wish was "more cake." Solid wish.

In the car I wanted a drink so badly my hands were shaking on the wheel. A little. I put on a podcast about bridges and drove the long way home. By the time I parked I was only tired. And proud. And annoyed that I'm proud of myself for sitting at a children's party.
