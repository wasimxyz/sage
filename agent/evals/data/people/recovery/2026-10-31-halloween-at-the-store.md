date: 2026-10-31
title: Halloween
---

A man dressed as a giant can of beer came in at noon for AA batteries. Yes. I heard it too. The costume had a pull tab. He asked where the batteries were and I said aisle four, past the rope, and watched him walk away with the pull tab bobbing. I'd like it noted that I did not react, and then I went to the stockroom and laughed with my face in a box of washers.

Kids came through all afternoon in costumes. Candy basket by the register. A very small ghost asked if we sold ghosts. Big Mike said no, and the ghost left satisfied, as though a deal had been struck.

Got home, turned off the porch light, ate the whole bowl. I'm calling it self-care and a sugar problem.
