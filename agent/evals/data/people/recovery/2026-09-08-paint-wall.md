title: Paint wall
---

The new regional manager showed up with a clipboard and a lanyard and said "we're going to reimagine the paint wall." It was a Tuesday. By "reimagine" he meant move all 1,200 sample chips to a different wall before Friday, and by "we" he meant me. I'm the shift lead. That's what the title is for, apparently, being the person "we" means.

Spent the day on a ladder with my arms full of colors. Eggshell, Oyster, Putty, Dune. Big Mike held the ladder and gave me updates on how I was doing at it, which I didn't need. I snapped at a customer about a brush. I apologized in the parking lot, to the customer, who was not there.

Home, nothing in the fridge, ate crackers. Felt the old pull toward the evening glass, the one that makes a day like this go quiet. Called it a pull, not a plan. Fine. Went to bed at nine.
