title: Day five

Called Ruth last night at 9:40 because I couldn't not. Said it fast, like pulling tape off. She was quiet for a second, then said "Okay. Thank you for telling me. Have you eaten?" I said no. She said eat something and come to the meeting tomorrow. I said I couldn't. She said "I'll sit next to you." She didn't say anything about the eighteen months, and she didn't say I should have called sooner. She asked what happened, and I told her, and she listened like it was a weather report. Not to judge it, just to know what was coming. She said the clock starts over but the eighteen months are still a thing that happened. I'm not sure I believe that yet.

Tonight I raised my hand and said "I'm day five. I drank last Saturday." Nobody gasped. Somebody said "welcome back," like I'd been on a trip. Luis hugged me long enough to get coffee on my sleeve. A woman I don't know slid me a white chip and said it's an ugly color, they all are. I hated that I wanted it. I took it.
