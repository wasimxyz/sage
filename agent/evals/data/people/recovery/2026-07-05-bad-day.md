title: Bad day
---

Bad day. No reason, which is its own special insult. Woke at five and lay there with the whole future sitting on my chest. Forty more years of this. The seltzer, the meetings, the careful walk past aisle nine. What's the use of being good at not drinking if nothing else gets better? I'm 38 and I live in a one-bedroom with a very nice couch and nobody to sit on it with me.

Called Mom and said nothing important for twenty minutes. Called Ruth and she didn't pick up, which is fair, it's Sunday. Did all the dishes. Went for a walk, and the walk felt like something a character does in a sad movie, so I came home and watched a different movie.

Around nine I ate cereal for dinner. Somewhere between the first bowl and the second the mood changed by itself. I don't trust it any more than I trusted the bad one. I'll take it.
