title: Fourteen months
---

Fourteen months this week. Ruth says to count in days if the months make me weird, so I did the days and it's 424. I work in a hardware store, I can count to 424.

At the grocery the cashier asked if I wanted a bag and I said "I'm fourteen months sober," because my brain skipped a step. She said "that's great, paper or plastic?" I said paper. I think we both handled it well.

Good day, really. Woke up without the old dread. Cleaned the entire kitchen because I could. Tomorrow I might hate everything, but tonight the counters are clean and I own a sponge with a handle.
