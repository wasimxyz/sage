title: Sunday at Mom's
---

Sunday dinner at Mom's. Pot roast, and she made too much, as always, enough for a lumberjack convention. She asked if I'm "still doing the thing" and I said yes, still doing the thing, and she said good in the voice of somebody who wants to say more and has been told not to.

Jess brought Milo, who is almost five and told me he's a dog now. I was the owner. I walked him around the yard on an invisible leash for twenty minutes. He got tired and lay down in the grass and wouldn't come inside until I said "good boy."

Mom put a bottle of sparkling cider by my plate, the fancy kind with the foil on the cork. She watched me open it. It's sweet that she buys it. It's also a small sad thing that it needs to be bought. I said it's the best cider I've had all year and she lit up like I'd handed her an award.
