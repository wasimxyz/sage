Fourteen days. Next to five hundred and fifty-something it looks like a toddler. Luis says the toddler turns into a big kid if I stop asking it to pay rent. I don't know what that means, but it's the kind of thing you'd put on a mug.

Morning: great, ate eggs, felt like a person who owns a calendar. Afternoon: sure I'd never be better than a person with good attendance and a bad habit. Evening: ate a whole pizza, watched half a movie, felt okay. Three different people lived in me today and none of them were consulted.
