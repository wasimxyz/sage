Thursday meeting at St. Anne's, which I'm writing up two days late. The coffee tastes like a hot penny but it's free and I'm loyal. Luis brought donuts, because somebody always brings donuts, like it's a legal requirement.

I raised my hand, said my name, said fourteen months, and then said the hard part isn't the not drinking, it's 5 to 9 every night, which used to hold a whole bottle and now holds a lot of television. People laughed the way you laugh when it's true. After, a new guy, maybe twenty-five, asked how I do it and I said "badly, every day, on purpose." Luis said that should be a mug.

Home by 9:30. Watched people turn shipping containers into houses. I wasn't thinking about anything, which was the point.
