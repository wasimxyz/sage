date: 2026-05-23
title: Aisle nine
---

Went in for milk and dish soap. The store, in its wisdom, puts the wine on the way to the dairy. I stopped in front of a red with a hand-painted sunset on the label. I would have killed for a glass of it. I don't mean that in a scary way, I mean light shoplifting at most. Maybe parking in a handicap spot.

Stood there four minutes by my phone. Read the back of the bottle. "Notes of dark cherry." It never had notes of cherry. It had notes of Tuesday. I texted Luis one word, "aisle nine," and he wrote back "leave the cart and walk to your car." I left half my groceries in the cart, so a stock boy somewhere hates me now. Sat in the car with the radio on. It passed, the way it does, like a wave you only see after it's gone.

Went back in and bought the milk. Felt like a hero for three hours, then felt stupid for three more, because it was only milk.
