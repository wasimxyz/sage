title: Voicemail
---

Ruth called twice yesterday and Luis once. I let all three go to voicemail. Went to work, sold a lot of caulk, smiled at everyone. Ate lunch in the car with the engine off.

The shame has an address. Just behind the sternum, like I swallowed a bar of soap. I keep rehearsing what I'll say. "So, funny story." There's no funny version. I tried one in my head and it didn't make it past the first line. Big Mike put a donut on the register next to me without saying anything and walked away. I would like to marry him.

Missed the Tuesday noon meeting. Haven't decided about Thursday. Writing this at 10 p.m. with the phone face down.
