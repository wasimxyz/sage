title: Bookshelf
---

Built a bookshelf from a box with 94 parts and a manual that has no words, only drawings of a very calm man. Put two shelves in backwards and found out at step 31. Took it all apart. Started again. The finished thing leans four degrees to the left, which I'm calling character. I put the books on it and it held.

Back when I drank I'd have started this at nine at night and ended with a hole in the wall and a feeling of betrayal about Sweden. Today I got a bookshelf. I stood back with my hands on my hips like a person at a ribbon cutting.
