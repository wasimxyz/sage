title: Twelve weeks
---

Twelve-week appointment with Dr. Shah. Iris weighs fourteen pounds two ounces and is in the seventy-fifth percentile for something, I was supposed to remember which. Dr. Shah says she's thriving, and I said "thriving" back to her like a parrot. She asked about sleep, and feeding, and tummy time, and then she asked how I'm doing. The way they do. She looked up from the screen to ask it, which is the thing. Not the clipboard way. And I had it right there, the whole mess of it, the stairs and the baths and the picture of her slipping, I had it in my mouth, and I said, "Tired. But good."

She nodded, kind of long. She said, "That's normal. If it ever feels like more than tired, you can tell me. Anything. Nothing shocks me." And I said thank you, and Iris started to scream, and the nurse came in with the shot, and I held her still and she looked at me like I'd betrayed her. Which, fine, I had. I cried in the parking lot. Not because of the shot.

I'm going to tell Kit this weekend. I mean it this time. I've said it before, but I mean it. I put it on the calendar, which is a joke, but I did.
