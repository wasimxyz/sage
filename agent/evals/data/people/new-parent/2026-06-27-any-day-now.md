title: Any day now
---

Everyone is texting "any day now." Tamsin texted "anything??" at nine, again at noon, again at four, with the little eyes emoji. Nothing. Walked to the end of the block and back and felt like a boat. Kit made soup that I ate standing up because sitting is its own project now. Thirty-eight weeks tomorrow. I told the bump to come out and it kicked me in the ribs. I'm choosing to read that as a yes.
