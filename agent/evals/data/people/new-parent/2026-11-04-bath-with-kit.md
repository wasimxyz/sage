title: Bath with Kit
---

I did the bath. With Kit right there. Kit sat on the closed toilet lid with a towel on their knee and didn't say anything, and I rolled up my sleeves and filled the baby tub, and I tested the water on my wrist, which at least is getting some use, and put her in.

I'd like to tell you it was a breakthrough. It wasn't. I held her with both hands the whole time, so hard my fingers cramped, and I counted my breathing, and the picture came, twice, a flash of it, the slip, the water. I said out loud, "There it is," and Kit said "okay," and I kept going. I washed her feet, which she likes. I did her hair with the little cup and she screamed, and then she stopped and looked surprised that she'd screamed. I was soaked to my elbows.

It was six minutes and it felt like an hour. When I lifted her out into the towel I realized I'd been holding my breath for what felt like the whole thing. I wrapped her up and handed her to Kit and then went and sat on the edge of our bed with my hands between my knees.

I don't feel better. I want to be honest in here at least. I don't feel cured and I'm not sure I'd do it alone tomorrow. Probably not tomorrow. Maybe on the weekend. Maybe not. Iris smelled like the baby soap and the bear towel, and when Kit brought her in she grinned at me with her whole face, soaking wet, like I'd done something wonderful, and I looked at her and thought, I'd do anything for you. I'd do six minutes. I'd do it a hundred times.

The support line number is still on the fridge. I called on Monday. Left a message. They haven't called back. Maybe they'll call tomorrow.
