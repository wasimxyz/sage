title: Pumpkin
---

Iris was a pumpkin. The costume came from a swap at Kit's school and it has a hat with a stem. She hated the hat for the first ninety seconds and then discovered the stem and stopped. Kit was a ghost, a sheet with two eye holes cut slightly too high. Four trick-or-treaters came, and we gave them each three pieces of candy and then ate the leftover candy ourselves, sitting on the porch step with the baby in her pumpkin outfit, rating the candy. I'd say the evening was a nine.
