title: The dream again
---

Had the dream again. I'll write it down because writing it makes it less of a thing, at least that's what I'm told. In the dream we're at the big grocery store, and I'm pushing the cart, and I'm doing the entire shop, produce, cheese, the aisle with the cat food even though we don't have a cat, and at the checkout I look in the cart for the baby and she isn't there. She's not in the cart. I look at the lady scanning my cheese and I say, "Where's the baby?" and the lady says, "What baby?" and I run back through the parking lot and the car is gone. Everything is bright and calm. That's the worst part. Nobody's upset.

Then I wake up. I always wake up at the exact second I'm running, and then I run for real, I'm in the nursery before I'm awake. Tonight she was in the crib on her back with both arms thrown above her head like she'd just been arrested, snoring. I stood there with my hand on her chest for a good five minutes. Her chest went up and down. The dream was just a dream.

I've had it four times now. Kit says it's very common, "new-parent brain," and brought me water, and went back to sleep in about nine seconds, which is the superpower I'd like most. I texted nobody. I don't know what I'd say. "Hi, I dreamed about the cheese aisle." Sorry to be rambling, I'm wide awake now. I might as well alphabetize something.
