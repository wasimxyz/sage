date: 2026-08-26
title: Who is this
---

I caught myself in the bathroom mirror at some terrible hour and for a second didn't know who it was. Not in a dramatic way. More like seeing a coworker out of context. There's a person there with my hair, but the hair has been slept on in a way that suggests commitment, and there's a stain on the shoulder that's either spit-up or yogurt or both. Tamsin sent me a picture from the office, the whole team at the new place on Fifth, the lunch spot I picked, and I looked at it for ten minutes. They're all there. They're all laughing about something. It's a whole world that kept going without me, which is exactly how it should work and also a little like walking past the window of a party you left and finding it's still going.

I used to finish things. That was my entire personality. I ran a half marathon last fall, I did the route 14 redesign (not finished, but I was going to), I was a person who read the end of a book. Today I finished half a sandwich and counted it. Yesterday I started a text to an old friend and I've been writing it for three days. It says "hey!!" so far.

And I'm not unhappy. I want to be clear about that, because that's what Kit gets worried about when I get quiet, and I tell Kit, I'm not unhappy, I'm just elsewhere. It's like I got moved to a different department. In this department the rules are different, there's no calendar, there are only two settings, which are asleep and screaming, and my badge doesn't work in the old building.

I don't know who I'm going to be on the other side. Everyone says "you'll find your feet," which people say about a lot of things, including bad haircuts.

Kit's taking the baby for a walk. I've got forty minutes. I'm going to lie on the floor of the nursery and look at the ceiling and not be useful. I have never been so good at it.
