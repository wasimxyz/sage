date: 2026-09-02
title: Kit goes back
---

Kit went back to school today. First day with the kids, so everybody's in new shoes, and Kit left at 6:40 with the good tote bag and a face like a person going to a funeral, even though it was Kit who said they were ready. I stood in the driveway with Iris on my hip and waved the way you'd wave at a ship. Then I came in, and the house was just enormous. It's the same house. It was full of sound all summer, Kit walking around, Kit talking to Iris in a robot voice, Kit running the dishwasher, and now there was the refrigerator hum and that was it.

I made a very ambitious schedule, a list in the notes app: 8, feed; 9, walk; 10, tummy time; 11, laundry. I was done with the entire list by 10:20. Then it was just the day. The whole giant day. I talked to Iris about the bus routes. I told her about the loop on Marlowe and why the 14 doesn't turn left at Fifth, and she looked at me with that wide serious face like she understood the municipal code, and I realized it was the first full conversation I'd had since Sunday. With a nine-week-old, who contributed a sneeze.

We walked the stroller for forty minutes. A neighbor I've never met nodded at me and I almost cried. Twelve people were on the trail and every one of them had somewhere to be. I felt like a ghost with a stroller.

Kit texted at 12:15, a picture of a card from a kid that said "WELCOME BACK TEACHER" with a drawing of what I think was a horse, and I laughed out loud in the kitchen. That's the thing: I'm so happy, and I'm so alone, and I didn't know you could be both at once at that volume. Kit's home at 4:30. I've been watching the clock since 2.
