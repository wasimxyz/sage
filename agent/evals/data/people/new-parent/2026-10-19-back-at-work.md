title: Back at work
---

First day back. I'd like to report that I was composed, and I will, and it's a lie. I walked in at 8:50 with a bag of pump parts and a laptop I didn't remember how to log into and the first thing anyone said to me was "Welcome back!" from Dennis, holding a balloon. A balloon, from the dollar store, that said WELCOME BACK and had already lost half its helium. I didn't say anything. It was weirdly touching and also like being handed a time capsule.

My password had expired, of course. IT reset it and it asked me a security question: "What was the name of your first pet?" I could not remember. It took me six tries. It was a rabbit named Noodle. I'd like it noted that I got there.

Tamsin hugged me for a long time. She smelled like the same hand cream. The route 14 loop got approved without me, which made me feel something I'm not going to name. I sat through four meetings. I understood about half of them. I pumped in a supply room that smelled of toner, with a sign on the door in Dennis's handwriting that said "OCCUPIED, PLEASE KNOCK," and I got a text from Mom with a picture of Iris asleep on her shoulder, and I looked at it, and I watched the bottle fill, and somebody knocked, and I said "one minute!" in a voice I haven't used in a year.

On the bus home I thought, I'm a person who can do this. And then I thought, I'm a person who can do this and miss her this much. I'd forgotten that the 14 smells like fries. It smelled like fries and I almost cried again.
