title: Blowout
---

Diaper blowout at the gas station on Route 9, which I'd like entered into the record as the most humiliating eleven minutes of my parenthood so far, and it's been a month, so there's competition. Here's how it went. We were coming back from the pediatrician (weight check, she's gaining like a champion), Kit was driving, I was in the back seat with Iris, and about two miles out of town there was a noise. You know the noise. A sort of wet, thoughtful noise. And then a smell, and Kit said, "Is that..." and I said "Pull over, pull over," and we pulled into the gas station, and it was up the back, up the onesie, into the car seat straps, onto my sleeve, and somehow into the hat. How does it get into the hat. The hat was on her head. I'd like to know the physics.

The gas station bathroom had no changing table. Kit held the baby up like a trophy, at arm's length, while I tried to wipe things with a paper towel the texture of sandpaper, and a man came in to wash his hands, looked at us, and said "congratulations" in the tone of someone who has been there. We threw the onesie away. We rinsed the car seat cover in the sink. I have never felt so competent and so defeated at once. Kit bought me a coffee from inside, the biggest size, and a bag of pretzels, and we drove home with all the windows down. Iris slept the whole way. She was the only one of us who'd put in a real effort.
