title: The death of me
---

I put the milk in the cabinet this morning and the cereal in the fridge, and I didn't notice until Kit opened the fridge at noon and said, "Is this on purpose?" I said Iris is going to be the death of me. I said it dramatically, with my arm over my forehead, and Kit laughed and said, "She'd be so sorry," and I said "She'd sleep through the funeral," and we went on like that for a while. They'll find me in the kitchen with a bottle in one hand and a single sock in the other and a note that says "should've gone to bed at 8." Obituary, one line: "Beloved. Never found the other sock."

Everyone jokes about it, everyone, it's how people get through the first months, I think. It's a kind of tired where you laugh because the alternative takes more energy. The keys were in the freezer yesterday. Also I found a diaper in the oven mitt drawer. I don't know.

Kit said, "She's the best thing that ever happened to us," and I said, "Both can be true," and Kit said, "They can." I think that's the most honest thing either of us has said in a month. Four hours total last night, in three pieces. I'm not complaining, I'm reporting. The cereal was fine, by the way. A little cold.
