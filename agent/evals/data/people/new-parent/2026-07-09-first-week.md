title: First week
---

One week. We've been home five days and I would like to report that I know nothing. Nothing. I know that the diaper log on the fridge has columns and that I've been filling it in wrong, I know that Iris eats for forty minutes, sleeps for ninety, and then wakes up like somebody pulled a fire alarm, and I know that the second I put her down in the bassinet she knows. She knows. She has a sensor in her back.

Kit and I took shifts at night, theoretically, except in practice it was me awake the whole time and Kit awake in a way that's more like lying down with their eyes open. I'm not complaining. Kit's been doing everything else: every meal, the laundry, the tiny hat she keeps losing, and the thing with the thermometer where I couldn't get a reading and almost called the nurse line and Kit just said, "Try the other ear," which worked. Somehow it's always the other ear.

My mom's coming Sunday. Three days, four maybe. She asked what I need and I said nothing, which isn't true, I need everything, I need someone to come in and tell me what day it is and stand in my kitchen and be competent, and also I don't want anyone in my kitchen. Both. I've gotten very good at wanting two things.

Dr. Shah said at the weight check that Iris is already back up to her birth weight, which she was very pleased about, and I felt like I'd passed a test I didn't know I was taking. Dr. Shah has a laugh like a sneeze. Iris slept the whole drive home. I drove like I was carrying a soufflé, twenty miles an hour, and a man on a bike passed me.

Also I locked myself out of my own phone because I couldn't remember my passcode. A passcode I've had for six years. I tried it three times and the phone said wait a minute and I sat in the nursery and cried about a four-digit number.
