title: Storytime
---

Went to baby storytime at the library, because the internet says socialization, and also because Kit said go, you'll feel better with other adults, and I said what if the other adults are the sort of parents who have a system, and Kit said "they're not." They were. Twelve babies on a rug and a librarian in a cardigan with a ukulele who sang about a spider, and all the babies stared at her like she was a television. Iris fell asleep immediately. I didn't get to do any of the hand movements. The woman next to me has a six-month-old who sleeps twelve hours, and she said it casually, like mentioning a nice day, and I'm not saying I hated her, but I did a lot of smiling.

There was a snack table, which was crackers, and a man with a baby in a carrier asked if I knew whether the swaddle thing was bad, because he'd read something, and everyone had an opinion about swaddles, and the swaddle discussion lasted longer than the singing. I said we use a sleep sack. Three people nodded with a lot of seriousness.

I got a library card for Iris. She's a cardholder now. The librarian said she could check out up to fifty books at a time, and I laughed, and then I got a little emotional about it, a person with their own library card. I took out one board book about trucks.

I did not make a friend. I said I'd go back next week. We'll see how Thursday goes.
