title: Quiet house
---

Mom left this morning and the house went quiet in that specific way where you can hear who isn't here. She stripped the guest bed and did a final load of burp cloths and left me a note on the fridge that says "FEED YOURSELF TOO. LOVE, MOM" and I stood there with my hand on the fridge for about a minute.

Also Iris has decided sleep is for the weak. Four months is coming, and apparently there's a thing, the four-month regression, where they sleep worse after finally sleeping a bit better, and a baby book that one of Kit's colleagues lent us calls it "a developmental leap," which sounds like the baby is going to physically jump somewhere. Last night: up at 12:40, 2:15, 3:30, 4:10, 5. I counted. I was a zombie at work yesterday, and nobody noticed, because everyone's a zombie.

The odd thing is that I was too tired to be scared. I did the 3:30 feed in the dark in the nursery, and the thing that normally comes with the dark didn't come, because my brain didn't have the energy for it. A weird kind of relief. I know it's not the same as it going away. I'm just noting it. Mom's cheese is in the fridge, it's very good, and I ate some standing at the counter at eleven at night with crackers.
