title: The stairs
---

Something happened today and I want to get it down before I talk myself out of it.

I was carrying Iris down the stairs. The front stairs, the steep ones, with the bad turn at the bottom. I do it every day. I had her against my shoulder with one hand under her and the other on the rail, and halfway down, completely out of nowhere, I saw it. Like a picture. Her slipping out of my arms and going down the steps. And then, worse, a half second where it was me, where my hands opened, like I had done it. I hadn't. I was holding on so tightly she squeaked. My heart was banging in my ears and I stopped on the third step from the bottom and stood there gripping the rail so hard my knuckles ached, and my legs were jelly, and I stayed there a long time, I don't know how long. Iris was fine. She was asleep. She had no idea.

I don't know where it came from. I don't want that. I want the exact opposite of that. I've never wanted anything as much as I want her to be okay, I'd do anything, so why does my brain make the film and press play. I kept saying I would never in my head, I would never, I would never, and the thing is I know that, I know it the way I know my own name, and it still made me feel sick. I felt like something was wrong with me.

I haven't said anything to Kit. Kit would be so kind about it, and then I'd have to feel it. I took the stairs again after, to prove I could, and held onto her with both arms and the rail in my teeth. Not literally. But almost.

I'm going to sit here a bit longer with her on my chest. She's warm. She's so warm. Her whole head fits in my hand.
