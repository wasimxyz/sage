title: First smile
---

She smiled at me. At me. Not a gas smile, I know what a gas smile looks like, I've seen forty. This one started in her eyes and went to the corner of her mouth and then the whole thing happened. I was changing a diaper. I said her name and she did it. Kit was in the shower so I yelled, and then I stopped yelling because I was afraid she'd stop. Kit missed it. I made a face to get it again for Kit and she did it again. It's the best thing that's happened this week. It might be the best thing that's happened this year.
