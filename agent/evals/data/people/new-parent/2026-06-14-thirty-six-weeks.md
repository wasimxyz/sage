Thirty-six weeks today and I have reorganized the spice rack twice, first alphabetical and then by cuisine, which Kit says is the most "me" thing I've ever done, and I said it's not nesting, it's inventory, and Kit just gave me that look. Anyway. The crib is built. Kit built it, then we read the instructions, then Kit un-built it because apparently one of the side rails was upside down, and now it's built again and I'm fairly sure it's correct but I keep going in there and pressing on it like a person checking a bridge.

The hospital bag has been packed since Tuesday. I've unpacked and repacked it four times. Slippers, the charger with the long cord (very important, Tamsin said, the outlets in those rooms are always behind the bed), two going-home outfits in two sizes because nobody knows how big this person is going to be, a fleece for me, snacks, and a list of the names we're still fighting over. Kit likes Iris. I like Iris but I also like Margot, and when I say Margot out loud I picture a smaller person, a precise person with a bob, and when I say Iris I picture someone barefoot in a field, and I can't decide which one I want to meet. Kit says we'll know when we see them. I said that's what everyone says about dogs.

Work was fine. I start leave on the 26th, officially, although I'll be honest, my route 14 redesign is not done and it's going to haunt me, because the council meeting is in August and Dennis isn't going to understand the loop on Marlowe unless I'm there to explain it. I said that to Tamsin and she said, "You are about to have a human being. Let Dennis figure out the loop."

Which is fair. It's also the last thing I'm in control of. I think I'm going to go alphabetize the freezer.
