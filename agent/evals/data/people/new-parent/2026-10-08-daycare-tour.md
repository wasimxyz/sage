title: Daycare tour
---

Toured the daycare on Maple, the one with the long waitlist. Twenty minutes with a woman whose lanyard said "Director," which is how people get named by their lanyards. Four rooms, one for babies, which smells exactly like what you'd imagine, a clean-sheets smell and an underneath smell. Cubbies with names on them, each in a different handwriting. A mobile of felt clouds. One of the teachers was holding a baby with one arm and writing on a clipboard with the other, and she looked at me and smiled, a real one, and I decided I trusted her without being able to explain it.

They have a spot starting October 26th. That's the Monday after Mom's second visit. It's a spot that opened because a family is moving to Phoenix, and I've been feeling guilty about being glad about somebody else's relocation. The Director said we'd need to bring labeled bottles, extra clothes, a sleep sack, "and a photo of the family for the wall." I said what for. She said, "So she can look at you when she misses you." And that was it. I walked to the car with my face doing something and pulled it together before I got to Kit.

Kit asked how it was and I said "fine, clean," and then I said "it's so expensive," and Kit said "yeah," and neither of us said the thing, which is that I'm going back to work in eleven days and I'm not ready, and somebody I haven't met is about to learn how Iris likes to be held. I started a list of everything I need to write down for them. It's three pages already. It began with her name and the temperature of her bottles and by the end I'd written how to hold her. I don't know how to write how to hold her. You just do it, and she looks at you.
