Sunday. Laundry mountain. I stood at the bottom of the basket and looked at it the way you look at a hill you're about to climb. There are tiny socks everywhere, I find them in my own coat pockets. Kit unloaded the dishwasher in a trance and put the bottle nipples in the utensil drawer and I put them back and we didn't discuss it. Iris learned to grab her own foot, so now she holds it with a focused expression like it's a delicate procedure. The thing about the washing machine is that I have, at this point, a relationship with it. It's the only thing in the house that does what I ask.

Nothing else to report. Ate leftover pasta standing up. Watched half a movie and slept through the other half with Iris on the couch cushion between us, like a third person who runs the show.
