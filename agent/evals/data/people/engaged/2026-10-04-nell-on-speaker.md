title: Nell on speaker
---

Sunday call with Jordan's grandmother, on speaker in his parents' kitchen while his mom stirred something. Nell is ninety-four and has a voice like a very friendly door hinge. She asked about the date. We said we haven't picked one. She said, "Well, pick soon, I'd like to see it before I go, ha!" and Jordan's mom said "Nell!" and Nell said "what, I'm being practical."

Then she talked for ten minutes about her tomatoes, which she's growing in buckets because bending over is "for the young." She walks to the post office. She's in better shape than most of us, and I would not bet against her at anything. I kept laughing at "before I go" for the rest of the night, because she said it the way you'd say "before I leave for work."

Jordan says she's been saying it since he was twelve. She's going to outlive the cake.
