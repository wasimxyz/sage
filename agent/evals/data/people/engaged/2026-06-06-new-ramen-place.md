title: New ramen place
---

Jordan and I tried the new ramen place down the street and it was a fifty minute wait! We sat on the bench outside next to a couple who were clearly on a first date, and they split an appetizer, which was adorable. We were probably unbearable in our own way, because we spent the whole wait guessing what the other one would order. I got his right. He got mine wrong and was very upset about it.

It was so worth it. The broth was incredible, the egg was perfect, and the table was the size of a cutting board, so we had to eat with our elbows in.
