title: Halloween party
---

Noor and Wes's Halloween party. Jordan and I went as the two halves of a pair of scissors, which meant we had to stand next to each other all night, and nobody got it until we explained, and then everybody said "oh" in a flat voice. A man dressed as a lamp won the contest. He earned it: the shade was on his head and the cord trailed behind him all evening.

Wes had turned the hallway into a tunnel with black garbage bags and a fog machine, and the fog machine set off the smoke alarm twice. The second time the neighbor from downstairs came up in his bathrobe, looked at all of us in our costumes, and just said "carry on."

We stayed until midnight, which is late for us. Jordan carried the scissors costume home over his shoulder like a rolled-up rug.
