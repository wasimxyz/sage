Put basil, mint and rosemary in pots on the balcony today and I'm weirdly excited about it! The balcony fits exactly two chairs as long as nobody is sitting in them, so the herbs went along the railing. Apparently herbs need water more than once a week, which is news to me. The mint is already trying to escape, so I gave it its own pot with a plate on top, like a tiny fortress. Wes says mint does that to everyone.
