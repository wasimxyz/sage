title: Leaf blower
---

The man across the street has been running a leaf blower since eight. He's moving the same leaves from one side of the lawn to the other. I've been watching from the balcony. It's like a performance. At ten he stopped, looked at the lawn, and started again from the opposite direction. I think he's an artist. Or he has a lot on his mind.
