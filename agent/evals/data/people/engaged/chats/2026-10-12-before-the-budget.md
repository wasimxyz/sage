User: I'm going to build a budget this week. Actually build one. I've been saying that for a month.
Sage: What's different this time?
User: I'm tired of checking the account three times a day. Noor's cousin runs a café and needs someone to redo her menus and fix the website, six hours a week at thirty an hour. I said yes before I could talk myself out of it.
Sage: Saying yes before the doubt arrives is a good trick. What do you hope the budget will do for you?
User: Tell me the truth. I think most of my panic is not knowing. The goal is five hundred in the cushion by January. First I'm cutting the $14 app subscription I forgot I had.
Sage: A number you can see is easier to carry than a fog. You can tell me how it goes if you feel like it.
