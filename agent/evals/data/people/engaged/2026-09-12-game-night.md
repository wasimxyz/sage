Game night at Noor and Wes's. We played the one with the little wooden cities and I lost so badly that I switched to a different game halfway through, which is not how it works, but nobody stopped me. Wes takes board games very seriously. He read the rule book out loud, in a voice, and we let him. Noor won, quietly, like she'd been planning it since the first turn.

Around ten Wes brought out the second game, the one with the cards, and Noor said "absolutely not" and put the box on top of the fridge where nobody could reach it without a chair. That's the third time. There's now a running joke about what's in it. I think it's just cards. Wes thinks it's a trap.

We ate three kinds of chips and a bowl of grapes that nobody admitted to buying. Jordan and I walked home. He said we should get a game table someday. I said where would it go, and he said "somewhere." We stood at the crosswalk and I could see him picking the spot.
